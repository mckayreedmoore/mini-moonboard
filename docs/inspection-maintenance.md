# Inspection and maintenance program

This is a release-gated record format, not an approved inspection interval or
structural assessment. The qualified reviewer must set the final inspection
scope, acceptance criteria, responsibilities, and intervals after the frame,
connections, floor interface, and impact area are designed.

## Before construction is released

The final program must identify:

- the responsible owner and the person qualified to inspect structural items;
- the applicable standard editions and reviewer-specified inspection intervals;
- every load-bearing member, connection, floor interface, and panel attachment;
- every hold, T-nut, LED, cable, control box, and power supply to be checked;
- the impact-attenuating surface and fall-zone arrangement; and
- the shutdown and repair authority if a defect is found.

## Commissioning record

Before climbing, record the approved commit/tag, installation date, reviewer,
as-built measurements, pad arrangement, and photographs of all sides and
connections. The reviewer must confirm that the built assembly matches the
released design or has approved every recorded deviation.

Do not commission the wall if the frame, connections, pads, electrical system,
or installation record is incomplete.

## User pre-climb check

Before use, the responsible user should confirm that:

- the crash pads and fall area are in their approved deployed arrangement,
  without gaps, displaced sections, or new obstructions;
- no panel, hold, fastener, support, or foot appears loose, damaged, wet,
  cracked, delaminated, or displaced; and
- no LED wiring, connector, control box, or power-supply component is exposed,
  damaged, or hot.

If any item is questionable, keep the wall out of service until the responsible
person and, where relevant, the reviewer evaluate it. Disconnect electrical
power before electrical inspection or repair, and follow the LED-system
manufacturer's instructions.

## Periodic and event-triggered inspection

The final reviewer-approved checklist must cover at least these groups:

| Group | Record observable condition and action |
| --- | --- |
| Climbing panels | Surface damage, splits, delamination, swelling, edge damage, mounting condition, seams, and insert integrity |
| Holds and hold hardware | Loose, spinning, damaged, or missing parts; do not invent torque values without the selected hardware specification |
| Frame and joints | Cracks, delamination, crushing, member movement, loose fasteners, connector damage, and changes to bracing |
| Feet and floor interface | Wear, sliding, indentation, uneven support, floor damage, and moisture; anchoring is not permitted in V1, and any future attachment requires a controlled scope revision |
| Impact surface | Coverage, pad gaps, compression damage, migration, moisture, tears, and obstruction-free fall zone |
| LEDs and electrical system | Damaged lights, wires, connectors, strain relief, control box, and power supply; use only the applicable official guide |

Perform an additional inspection after relocation, a significant impact or
collision, water exposure, a suspected overload, a new structural noise or
movement, any alteration, or an extended period out of use.

Link every corrective action to a record in
[`change-control.md`](change-control.md). A repair that alters any structural
or controlled component must follow the same review process as a design change.
