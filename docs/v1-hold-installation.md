# V1 Mini hold-configuration installation

The frame is independent of the hold configuration. It accepts either the
Mini 2020 or Mini 2025 official layout on the same 142 drilled 3/8-16 insert
positions and 132 LED locations. Changing configurations never changes the
panel envelope, rails, legs, structural fasteners, or structural analysis.

The current purchasing default is the user-owned [Mini MoonBoard 2025 Setup
Hold Bundle, SKU 60-105-2025](https://us.moonclimbing.com/products/mini-moonboard-2025-hold-set):
138 holds comprising Original School Holds, School Holds Set F, and Wood Holds
B/C. Moon states that bolts, T-nuts, and LEDs are excluded. The 2020 bundle is
also compatible with this frame, but its app-directed layout and bolt inventory
are different.

## Purchase and inventory sequence

1. Buy one `60-105-2025` hold bundle and two selected Escape 100-packs. Do not
   buy a single bulk bolt length before the hold boxes arrive.
2. Make an inventory table of all 138 holds: manufacturer identifier, official
   setup, orientation mark, counterbore type/depth, bolt-hole thread, and any
   pin-screw hole. The official MoonBoard app/setup is the authority for the
   final position and orientation, not a hand-drawn approximation.
3. With an actual 18 mm panel offcut, fit each distinct hold family to the
   selected Escape 3/8-16 insert. Select a 3/8-16 bolt that fully engages the
   insert without bottoming out; record the resulting quantity by length.
4. Use a pin screw only where the received hold provides that hole, and fit-test
   its path against the panel, T-nut, LED, cable, and support-side service gap.
   Never let a pin screw become a substitute for the hold bolt. Select screw
   length from the actual hold and offcut rather than carrying over 2020 advice.
5. Install with a hand tool or low-torque drill; Moon advises against an impact
   driver for Original School Holds. Retain the completed inventory as the
   controlled bolt purchase record.

The 2025 layout leaves four of the 142 V1 T-nut locations unused; keep those
capped/empty. This plan does not model hold solids because Moon does not publish
downloadable CAD for these Mini hold sets and their actual profile/bolt-depth
envelope comes from the received hold set. Do not substitute inferred or scanned
hold geometry into the structural model; it is only useful later for detailed
collision and bolt-length checks.
