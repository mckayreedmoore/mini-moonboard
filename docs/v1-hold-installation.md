# V1 official Mini 2020 hold installation

V1 selects Moon Climbing's [Mini MoonBoard 2020 Setup Hold Bundle, SKU
60-105-2020](https://us.moonclimbing.com/products/mini-moonboard-2020-hold-set).
Moon describes it as the complete official 130-hold Mini 2020 configuration:
Original School Holds plus Wood Holds A, B, and C. The same listing explicitly
excludes bolts, T-nuts, and LEDs. This document separates those purchase facts
from the board's 142 available 3/8-16 T-nut positions.

## Purchase and inventory sequence

1. Buy one `60-105-2020` hold bundle and two selected Escape 100-packs. Do not
   buy a single bulk bolt length before the hold boxes arrive.
2. Make an inventory table of all 130 holds: manufacturer identifier, official
   orientation mark, counterbore type/depth, bolt-hole thread, and any pin-screw
   hole. The official MoonBoard app/setup is the authority for the final
   position and orientation, not a hand-drawn approximation.
3. With an actual 18 mm panel offcut, fit each distinct hold family to the
   selected Escape 3/8-16 insert. Select a 3/8-16 bolt that fully engages the
   insert without bottoming out; record the resulting quantity by length.
4. Moon's Original School Hold guidance advises pinning applicable bolt-on holds
   with approximately 5/32 or 3/16 in wood screws. Moon's Wood Hold A guidance
   calls for a mix of 50 and 60 mm wood screws. Use a pin screw only where the
   received hold provides that hole, and fit-test its path against the panel,
   T-nut, LED, cable, and support-side service gap. Never let a pin screw become
   a substitute for the hold bolt.
5. Install with a hand tool or low-torque drill; Moon advises against an impact
   driver for Original School Holds. Retain the completed inventory as the
   controlled bolt purchase record.

The 12 unused V1 T-nut locations remain capped/empty unless a future controlled
hold-layout revision changes the official setup. This plan does not model hold
solids because their actual profile and bolt-depth envelope come from the
received hold set.
