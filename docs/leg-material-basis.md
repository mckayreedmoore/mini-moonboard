# Published material basis for the support-leg candidates

Material selection is a design task, not missing information the owner must
supply. For a new build, use the specifications below as the purchasing and
calculation basis. These choices do not select either candidate for construction.

| Component | Specified basis | Published source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single 2×6 leg and associated 2×6 receiver | US Douglas Fir–Larch No. 2, grade-stamped, dry, unincised; nominal 2×6, actual 1½ × 5½ inches | [AWC 2024 NDS Supplement](https://awc.org/resources/2024-nds-supplement/), Table 4A and applicable size adjustments |
| Wider candidate stock | Same species/grade/service basis; nominal 2×8, actual 1½ × 7¼ inches | Same NDS source, with 2×8 size factors |
| ⅜-inch bolt candidate | PECO **38X5HBG5USSZ**, ⅜-16 × 5-inch Grade 5 zinc-plated hex cap screw; catalog specifies 1-inch thread | [PECO Grade 5 catalog](https://www.pecofasteners.com/Category/Items/233?webdesc=Grade+5+USS+Zinc+Plated) |
| ½-inch bolt candidate | PECO **12X5HBG5USSZ**, ½-13 × 5-inch Grade 5 zinc-plated hex cap screw; catalog specifies 1¼-inch thread | [PECO exact product](https://www.pecofasteners.com/details/item?itemid=12X5HBG5USSZ) |
| Bolt steel | SAE J429 Grade 5; 92 ksi minimum yield, 120 ksi minimum tensile strength at these diameters | [Portland Bolt's SAE J429 specification](https://www.portlandbolt.com/technical/specifications/sae-j429/) |
| Nuts | Matching thread and SAE J995 Grade 5 | Existing candidate hardware requirements |

The timber calculations already use the specified species and grade. Generic
ungraded lumber, another species combination or incised stock is not an
automatic substitute. Similarly, a fully threaded tap bolt is not the selected
partially threaded cap screw.

The Grade 5 property and published thread length are available online; they
should not continue to appear as unanswered owner questions. The historical
thread-root calculations remain useful comparisons, but do not represent the
chosen new smooth-shank fastener solely because an owner has not supplied a
bolt specification.

Thread runout, total grip, plates, washers, nut seating and projection must be
checked as a complete stack. The preceding ⅜-inch hardware candidate has its
own modeled plate/spacer geometry; naming a catalog bolt does not qualify or
make that whole candidate fabrication-free. The ½-inch stack is documented in
[the wider-leg hardware schedule](wider-leg-hardware.md).

The missing published yield floor for the selected Simpson BP1/2 plate is a
specific exception to the otherwise available material specifications. It is
not a reason to ask the owner to select bolt or lumber grades. Resolving that
plate selection remains a design/sourcing task.

What these specifications cannot establish is the assembled joint's load
sharing, rotational stiffness, contact and prying response. Those require
connection analysis or suitable experimental evidence. Ordinary checking that
delivered goods match the specified product is a construction receiving check,
not a prerequisite for completing the design or another owner clarification.
