# Engineering branch decision after first uncut case

Date: September 14, 2026.

## Decision

Keep `compact-floor-flush-development` as selected authority. Continue
`compact-floor-uncut-development` for exactly one bounded rim-bevel method
investigation. Do not promote it, run its remaining five cases, complete its
hardware, or revise its connections before that investigation ends.

This choice advances the option that removed one known local-method problem
without transferring acceptance from another assembly. It is not a design
selection or construction release.

## Evidence controlling the branch

The accepted no-slip uncut A12-left response converged in 11 contact cycles and
meets all 25 listed conditional criteria in its portable assessment. This makes
its member and connection actions useful for the next bounded investigation.
It does not close limitations expressly excluded from those criteria.

Compared with the selected flush frame, the uncut arrangement removes the rear
leg's 1:12 side recess and its unresolved sawn-lumber transverse-tension method.
The uncut arrangement retains a full horizontal rim bevel. Its first recovered
full section has left-rim q+ compression, while the right rim reaches 0.017573
MPa q+ tension. That result does not prove local failure, but it prevents using
the conditional compression-face route for both sides without further support.

The branch also retains two independent connection gates. Its proposed front
bolt/washer stack lacks a guaranteed independent-bore registration envelope,
catalog-wide smooth-body coverage, and a published through-thickness washer
resistance basis. Its commercial angles retain unlisted separation and
independent moment demands; the current generic SDS route does not qualify the
complete wrench. Additional global cases cannot resolve either missing method.

## Bounded next question

Task 5b asks whether one primary, material-compatible method covers the existing
full horizontal bevel under the accepted A12-left geometry, bearing and combined
actions. It ends with either:

1. a reproducible applicable resistance comparison with all method conditions
   identified; or
2. a supported rejection naming the exact missing resistance input and one
   finite alternative geometry or method study.

No new structural solve is part of task 5b. A negative result stops the uncut
branch before contact refinement or the remaining load cases. A positive result
allows the coordinator to decide whether connection preflight should precede
contact/mesh work. Selection remains unchanged unless the owner later requests
promotion after applicable completion gates are met.
