# Hardware for the tapered floor-runner trial

**Historical design record.** Selections, “current” cases and outstanding
work below refer to the named earlier candidate. The [selected baseline](README.md)
and [active bolted-frame development](bolted-frame-development/README.md)
retain their own geometry, hardware and evidence.

The tapered candidate retains the square-recess trial's twelve complete bolt
stacks: two half-inch upper bolts per leg, two three-eighths-inch front bolts
per runner and two three-eighths-inch rear bolts per runner. Retain the original
56 mm upper pattern; the selected exterior's 64 mm drilling does not transfer.

Use the [recess hardware requirements](floor-runner-recess-hardware.md): rear
bolts are 4½ inches long across an 88.9 mm wood grip; front bolts are 4 inches
across 76.2 mm. Inspect actual shank coverage, washer dimensions, nut seating,
runout and complete exposed threads. Keep nuts and tips outward. The tapered
runout adds no screws, plates or composite-action credit.

Hardware feasibility does not qualify the tapered leg. Use its own
[assessment and status](history/floor-runner-taper-study.md) and generated schedules.
