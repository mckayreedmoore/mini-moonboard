## Simpson Strong-Tie — A66

Subject: A66 through-bolt data for a demountable timber connection

I am developing an independent freestanding indoor climbing-frame design and evaluating
the US A66 angle as an unmodified factory connector. Some receiving members are actual
1.5 × 5.5-inch Douglas Fir–Larch No. 2 lumber; proposed bolts would enter the broad face.
The 2026 catalog lists two 3/8-inch bolts per leg but does not publish A66 F1/F2 loads.

Does a supported installation exist for this member thickness and connection arrangement?
Please identify a controlled dimensioned factory hole/bend/edge drawing, applicable material
specification and minimum base-metal thickness, manufacturing tolerances, installation
restrictions, and any applicable test or design information for independent assessment.
I am not transferring another angle's steel grade or a nailed rating to A66.

If A66 is unsuitable or the information is unavailable, is there a better-documented
unmodified factory connector or arrangement worth investigating? Please distinguish a
product-supported installation from information supplied only for independent engineering.
This request does not ask Simpson to certify the entire frame.

## ABB — ZAB205EG-10 / AB205EG

Subject: ZAB205EG-10 / AB205EG material and fitting data

I am evaluating an unmodified AB205 fitting in an independently engineered demountable
timber connection. I am not treating the published supported-strut-channel load as a
timber-joint rating.

Please confirm whether the retail ZAB205EG-10 and the AB205EG drawing identify the same
current fitting. Please identify its material specification, guaranteed strength basis,
minimum base-metal thickness, controlled hole/bend/edge geometry, tolerances, restrictions,
and any fitting-level test or design data suitable for independent assessment. The proposal
uses through-bolts with actual 1.5-inch-thick timber; a representative detail can be supplied.

If this use is outside the intended application or those data are unavailable, is there
a more suitable documented factory fitting? This request does not seek approval of the
entire frame or extrapolate the 2,000-lb channel configuration.
