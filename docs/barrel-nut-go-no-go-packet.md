## Gate 1 — owner-required two-bolt principal/header joint

This is the first and governing barrel decision. The applicable stations are
`clip_split_base_center_left` and `clip_split_base_center_right`, joining
`base_header` to `base_principal_center_left/right`. They govern the next
step because they combine the only one-bolt duties, the only demonstrated
local face-normal twist mode, the thinnest recorded head-pocket edge stock,
an oblique bolt/barrel path, and a deeply recessed barrel service path.

Current maintained-pose facts for each side are:

- one 114.3 mm bolt;
- isolated rank 5/6 with a free rotation aligned to the face normal;
- 2.092152 mm minimum nominal header pocket stock;
- 14.0462 mm nominal principal barrel-edge ligament;
- 5,055.451 mm² nominal trial-cut contact face, but only 588.180 mm² behind
  the bolt line;
- selected CDE `1472BHT5` 4.5-inch bolt geometry gives 9.728 mm adverse
  physical-tip reach past the modeled barrel axis and 7.188 mm after the
  same conservative two-pitch incomplete-thread allowance used elsewhere;
  those values extend 4.724 mm and 2.184 mm, respectively, past the nominal
  5.0038 mm barrel radius, but do not establish usable female-thread overlap;
- no accepted signed barrel-case demand or connected-restraint result.

**Decision for that pose: `NO_GO`.** The owner requires two bolts per side,
and the isolated one-bolt joint also leaves face-normal twist unrestrained.
Connected-frame rank under assumed closed contacts cannot waive either fact.

The prior 50-degree two-row trial is also rejected rather than adopted. It
leaves only 2.7962 mm nominal principal wood beside a barrel, 1.501 mm
nominal stock between 21 mm header pockets, and 2.092152 mm nominal header
edge stock. Its right-hand bolt paths also intersect the retained 38.1 mm
F1–G1 electrical-service bore. None of those nominal stocks includes hardware,
lumber, drilling, or datum tolerances. Do not reuse that pose or its old
two-row component arithmetic.

The direct cross-dowel approach therefore remains `EVIDENCE_BLOCKED`, not
rejected as a connector family. Its next bounded artifact must be one new
source-built two-bolt pose per side. It may enter exploratory response work
only when all of these conditions pass:

1. **Two-direction restraint.** Two separated bolt/barrel axes provide a
   physical couple for both signs of face-normal twist. Check face opening and
   closure separately: an opening face supplies no tension or friction;
   closure may use only bounded compression contact. Demonstrate the connected
   path for both signs of normal force, both twist signs, and both lateral
   signs. A two-point group is not automatically a complete six-degree-of-
   freedom restraint or twice one fastener's resistance.
2. **Adverse-cut wood.** Build combined solids from the actual header and both
   principals, including head pockets, shaft bores, barrel bores, service
   cuts, contact bevels, and the retained F1–G1 passage. Use maximum barrel,
   washer, pocket, and bore sizes; minimum delivered timber sections; and
   adverse correlated position/angular errors. Report continuously searched
   minimum header edge stock, inter-pocket stock, principal barrel-side/end
   ligaments, cut net sections, and contact-face remnants. Every minimum must
   stay positive and then pass an applicable splitting, breakout, bearing,
   and combined-section resistance check; positive geometry alone is not a
   strength pass.
3. **Installed stack.** Recompute length from the new seat to each actual
   thread axis. Bind each row to a controlled CDE bolt SKU and measured lot,
   selected Fastenal `33857` washer, and mapped STAFAST barrel. For every row,
   measured complete male/female overlap must meet a demand-supported
   engagement requirement and retain accepted positive tip clearance. The
   current 4.5-inch arithmetic is a lead, not acceptance for a moved row.
4. **Installation and service.** With adjacent members and fixed panel layout
   represented, demonstrate drilling, barrel insertion/orientation, finger
   start, washer support, driver engagement and swing, tightening, inspection,
   bolt removal, barrel extraction, and non-damaging reassembly for both rows.
   The present greater-than-41 mm recessed insertion path and 2.092 mm edge
   zone have not passed that rehearsal.
5. **Evidence route.** Obtain CDE lot certificates/Class 2A confirmation,
   Fastenal washer lot evidence, and either controlled STAFAST tolerance,
   complete-thread, material, and resistance data or lot-specific hardware
   tests. No current source supplies a transferable STAFAST proof/strip/wall
   resistance or a complete wood-joint rating.

Minimum remaining wood cannot yet be reported for a realizable new pose
because the new axes and accepted process tolerances do not exist. For each
candidate pose, calculate rather than assume:

`edge stock min = measured edge-to-axis min - maximum pocket radius`

`inter-pocket stock min = minimum axis pitch - both maximum pocket radii`

`barrel ligament min = measured boundary-to-axis min - maximum barrel radius`

The measured terms must already include stock undersize and adverse correlated
datum, location, and angular error. Zero error is not an allowed input.

### Decisive representative test

After nominal and adverse geometry pass, fabricate no frame parts yet. Freeze
one representative right-hand full-section header/principal test joint with
the retained service cut and adverse accepted pocket/bore geometry; it may
envelope the left only if the geometry report proves that relation. Record
actual hardware, engagement, wood grade/density/moisture, cuts, tightening,
and assembly cycles. Measure relative member motion independently of fixture
travel.

First use an exploratory specimen to obtain lower and upper stiffness/dead-zone
bounds for axial separation/closure, both lateral directions, and both signs
of face-normal twist. Cycle through opening, reversal, reclosure, bolt removal,
and the declared reassembly count. This specimen may reject the pose but may
not qualify it. Use those bounds for the six fresh signed cases; then freeze
their simultaneous force/moment envelopes and run a predeclared multi-specimen
complete-joint qualification with the same adverse geometry and hardware lot.
Acceptance must cover bolt interaction, male/female thread strip, barrel wall
and flexure, washer/head seat, wood bearing/splitting/breakout/net section,
contact opening, slip, permanent set, and stiffness in both signs.

The new pose is `NO_GO` if it cannot fit two independently acting rows with
positive adverse stock and usable access, if either twist sign lacks a real
reaction path, or if the exploratory joint fails below the predeclared screen
load. It is `EVIDENCE_BLOCKED` while dimensions, supplier evidence, or the
representative test are missing. It can become `GO_TO_RESPONSE` only after
the five pre-response conditions above pass; later qualification still
depends on fresh signed demand. A plain through-bolted timber cleat remains a
fallback only after one of these defined direct-dowel gates fails.

- bolt under-head length, first and last complete male thread, transition,
  runout, tip/chamfer, thread class, material minimum, and lot;
- barrel insertion end, OD extrema, length, thread-axis location, first and

- finished seat-to-axis distance, bore depth, position error, angular drift,

Calculate usable overlap for each pair as

`max(0, min(last male, last female) - max(first male, first female))`.

least the required effective engagement from an accepted resistance method and
its worst-case bottoming clearance meets the accepted positive allowance.

## Gate 3 — finished barrel-bore fit and adverse wood

Current barrel OD and insertion-bore diameter are both 10.0076 mm, so modeled
diametral allowance is zero. A 13/32-inch bit is only a tool lead; it is not an
accepted fit.

Freeze and record:

- barrel OD minimum/maximum, roundness, and axis tolerance;
- finished bore minimum/maximum after the actual bit, guide, stock, moisture,
  feed, and backing process;
- center, depth, and angular tolerances for every shaft bore, insertion bore,
  head pocket, and service cut;

The acceptance calculation must report minimum and maximum diametral
clearance, intersection-axis error, insertion/retention behavior, and one
realizable adverse combined-cut solid per tolerance state. Every allowed
state must remain a valid connected solid and pass continuous or conservative

`EVIDENCE_BLOCKED`; zero tolerance may not substitute.

Use checklist trials T-1 through T-3 for fit/process data, T-4 for the
selected 6.5-inch stack, and T-7 for the principal/header pocket. These are
unloaded fit trials, not strength qualification.

## Finite stop and decision rule

Stop at `EVIDENCE_BLOCKED` if the controlled document packet, owner
measurements, unloaded offcut trials, or tool/assembly rehearsal are not
available. Do not compensate by adding solver runs or generic criteria.

Issue `GO_TO_RESPONSE` only when all four pre-response gates above have
positive adverse-tolerance geometry, bounded response inputs, and complete
resistance routes. Then run the six fresh signed cases and issue
`GO_TO_QUALIFICATION` only if signed twist, engagement, local resistance, and
serviceability all pass. A failed fixed joint geometry or required physical
path is `NO_GO` for the current pose.

Unavailable or undersized owned plywood is an owner supply/measurement block,
not structural `NO_GO`, while a qualifying sheet or offcut could still be
obtained. Panel yield becomes `NO_GO` only if the fixed six outlines cannot fit
any accepted measured stock and nesting without changing the fixed geometry.
Neither staged GO is a DIY or climbing release.

After `GO_TO_QUALIFICATION`, close any required complete-joint physical
qualification, retained-bolt and panel resistance, then produce the matching
shop packet and independent mechanics and shop reviews.
