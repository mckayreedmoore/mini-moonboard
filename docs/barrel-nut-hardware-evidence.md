# Barrel-nut hardware evidence ledger

The machine-readable BN-1 ledger is
[`barrel-nut-hardware-evidence.json`](barrel-nut-hardware-evidence.json). It
covers all 46 provisional bolt/barrel/washer stacks in the integrated
kerf-right candidate and the twelve retained through-bolt stacks, grouped into
three retained hardware families. It is an evidence inventory, not a product
selection, drilling schedule, capacity claim, purchase list, or fabrication
release.

Each field distinguishes controlled product evidence, exact retail claims,
repository requirements, modeled derivations, comparisons, conflicts, missing
evidence, pending receiving inspection, and candidate-specific rechecks. The
ledger deliberately keeps modeled shaft reach separate from usable thread
engagement and keeps the retained baseline catalog basis separate from its
required recheck in the changed frame.

## Current BN-1 result

The documentary gate remains blocked. Hillman 880543 has a usable retail
identity and nominal body dimensions, but no reviewed controlled drawing fixes
its axis, tolerances, lead-in, complete female-thread span, or usable depth. No
traceable minimum material, stripping, barrel-wall, or part-specific resistance
evidence applies to that SKU. The four bolt-length leads also lack enough
controlled thread/runout/tolerance information to compute minimum engagement
across the installed stack.

The model adds three immediate fit blockers. Twelve 6-inch rail bolts pass only
1.849 mm beyond the assumed barrel axis. The approved 2 mm machine-bore
extensions give the four 5-inch top-outer bolts 2 mm nominal tip clearance,
but controlled hardware and drilling tolerances do not yet prove a positive
worst-case gap. All 46 barrel insertion bores still have zero nominal
diametral allowance. No head-side washer is selected or qualified for geometry
and bearing.

Fourteen modeled tips also pass the provisional barrel far wall: two
base-center bolts by 7.6452 mm, four center-post/header bolts by 5.2452 mm,
four outer-header/post bolts by 6.1962 mm, and four top-outer bolts by 1.4452
mm. That can work only if controlled through-thread, exit, bottoming, and bore
geometry supports it. Retail orientation instructions and a nominal rim sweep
do not replace physical insertion, alignment, extraction, reassembly, and
stuck-fastener recovery evidence.

The retained Bolt Depot products have a materially better documentary basis:
exact Grade 5 bolt, nut, and washer references and baseline fit thresholds are
recorded. They still need candidate-specific grip, hole, demand, and resistance
checks because the barrel candidate changes hidden framing. Actual on-hand
parts and lumber also remain uninspected.

BN-1 can close only after exact controlled or measured-lot inputs make each
family's minimum thread overlap and full resistance path calculable, or after a
predeclared complete-joint qualification route supplies the missing basis.
Receiving inspection remains a separate owner action and must not be marked
complete from documentary research.

## RAMPA Type Q development comparison

[RAMPA Type Q package 0206161010100](https://www.rampa.com/na/en/RAMPA-CROSS-DOWELS-TYPE-Q/0206161010100)
and the
[manufacturer catalog](https://www.rampa.com/media/b2/92/cb/1679059847/LP_Canada_alleSeiten_letter.pdf)
identify article 020 616 101 as a slotted, zinc-plated-steel, 1/4-20 UNC
cross-dowel with nominal 10 mm diameter, 16 mm length, and thread axis 6 mm
from the catalog datum end. It is a better documented development lead than
Hillman 880543, but it is not a drop-in or qualified structural selection.

Current candidate CAD assumes a 10.0076 × 16.002 mm Hillman-shaped body with
its axis centered 8.001 mm from either end. Preserving the current bolt axis
with the RAMPA part would require a declared insertion orientation and a
2.001 mm body-position change, followed by fresh cut, ligament, tool, and
service queries. The nominal 10 mm body also supplies no controlled finished
bore allowance.

No public part-specific Type Q tolerance, complete usable female-thread
interval, material mechanical minimum, proof/stripping resistance,
barrel-wall resistance, or lot conformity basis was found. RAMPA's
[ETA scope](https://www.rampa.com/eu/en/products/eta) names other insert
families and does not transfer to Type Q. Hillman's
[retail-hosted dimensions](https://www.lowes.com/questions/hillman-880543-specialty-nuts/3012559/0d07d7d4-94c9-59c7-b300-30a2fd0be7bd)
likewise do not supply those controlled fields. Freeze neither SKU until the
finite document or lot-specific qualification packet is complete.
