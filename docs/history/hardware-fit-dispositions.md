# Hardware fit: current candidate dispositions

Applies to **top-joint-development**, retaining its selected-product predecessor
as a separate model. This checklist consolidates existing evidence; it is **not
construction approval**, a machining release or a resistance calculation.
“Published” below means documented catalog geometry, not measured delivered fit.

## Close the checklist efficiently

1. **Product identification:** collect delivered fastener markings/dimensions,
   US A21 drawing or measurements, hold-seat inventory, and lighting labels and
   connector/retention details. No offcut-test prerequisite is reinstated.
2. **Drawing decisions:** replace only the affected proxy/allowance geometry,
   finalize actual head seats and fabrication details, and rerun local clearance
   and service checks. Preserve the official hold/LED grid and predecessor models.
3. **Installation and structural review:** specify tightening/locking/inspection
   practice and safe temporary support. Rib screws precede skins; obstructed edge
   screws precede legs. These access dependencies are not an erection procedure.
   Resolve joint resistance and unanchored stability separately from fit.
