# Hold scanning and publication policy

This is risk-management guidance, not legal advice. It applies to the
user-owned Mini 2025 holds and any third-party hold.

## Project rule

Do not commit, publish, distribute, sell, or upload a faithful scan, printable
mesh, CAD solid, or derivative model of a Moon hold without Moon Climbing's
written permission that expressly covers the intended release and license.
Keep any private fit-check scan outside this repository (`scans/` is ignored),
without Moon logos, marks, artwork, or app-layout data, and do not use it to
make replacement holds. A private scan can support a local clearance or
bolt-length check, but it is not an authorization to reproduce the hold.

## Why this is the conservative boundary

The shape has a practical climbing function, but that does not make a faithful
public copy automatically safe. In the United States, copyright can protect
original separable sculptural/artistic features of a useful article; the answer
is fact-specific. A scan could also implicate design-patent, trademark,
trade-dress, contract, consumer-protection, and platform-policy issues,
especially if it preserves Moon branding or is offered as a Moon-compatible
replacement. Moon's public terms include its general site disclaimer and an
all-rights-reserved copyright notice; they do not grant a scanning or
redistribution license.

The MakerWorld foothold link supplied during research could not be retrieved
from this environment, so this project does not rely on its author, license,
accuracy, or permission status. A file being publicly downloadable elsewhere
is not evidence that it may be copied or re-uploaded.

## Safe path if publication is wanted

Ask Moon Climbing in writing before scanning. State: the exact hold set and
identifiers, whether the capture is a faithful mesh or simplified envelope, the
purpose (for example, non-printable viewer collision visualization), every
destination, whether downloads or printing are enabled, the proposed license,
attribution, and whether commercial use is prohibited. Keep the written reply
with the scan. For any commercial release, replacement-hold use, or unclear
reply, obtain advice from an IP lawyer in the jurisdiction of release.

Use a clean-room, original parametric **envelope** only if a reviewer confirms
it is not recognizably derived from a particular hold and it cannot function as
a replacement. It still must not use Moon/MoonBoard names or logos in a way
that implies sponsorship.
