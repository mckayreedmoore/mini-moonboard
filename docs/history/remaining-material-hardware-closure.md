# Remaining material and non-frame hardware decisions

Selection basis, checked 2026-09-06; **no purchases, CAD changes or strength
approval**. The purchased [Roseburg AC face sheets](purchased-materials.md)
remain face material, not an implied leg-plywood purchase. The user's offcut-test
waiver is unchanged; the identification and dimensional checks below do not
reinstate an offcut-test prerequisite.

## Leg, splice and cheek plywood

Select **Roseburg AB Marine, nominal 3/4-inch, seven-ply, APA-trademarked PS 1**
plywood as the procurement family for the remaining structural plywood profiles.
Roseburg identifies this construction on its [marine-core page](https://www.roseburg.com/softwood-plywood/marine-core/).
Specify the AB product, not BB Marine, AC face stock or an undocumented
“marine” substitute. Obtain the distributor's exact item, delivered stamp,
thickness tolerance and directional panel design basis before assigning values.
Marine grade is not a rating for this drilled leg or its mechanical joints.

Retain **two independently acting nominal 3/4-inch leg plies**, with no
adhesive/friction/composite credit. The model's 19.05 + 19.05 = 38.10 mm is a
nominal stack, not guaranteed delivered thickness. Two 23/32-category sheets
instead total 36.5125 mm by category alone and are not interchangeable without
updating grips, spacers and geometry. Keep the intended grain/strength-axis
orientation in the profile nesting. Final sheet count follows usable stock
dimensions and nesting; the bounding blanks are not a cutting yield guarantee.

The later [leg-bolt resistance checkpoint](leg-bolt-resistance.md) and coupled
FE trial temporarily idealize bonded plies to diagnose the connection. They do
not supersede this no-composite-credit procurement/design basis or qualify the
glue. Individual-ply bearing and stitch transfer remain unresolved under this
basis; the conditional laminate reference must not be used as its allowable.

## Holds and inserts

Retain the owned [Mini 2025 bundle, 60-105-2025](https://us.moonclimbing.com/products/mini-moonboard-2025-hold-set)
and the measured Escape **3/8-16** inserts. The 138-hold bundle excludes bolts
and T-nuts. Select climbing-hold bolts with that thread, matched to each actual
hold seat; never substitute M10 or the frame's A307 hex bolts.

Exact head/length quantities remain a hold-specific input. The
[US wood-bolt kit page](https://us.moonclimbing.com/products/moonboard-wood-hold-bolts)
currently describes 32 flat-head 3.5-inch bolts; that does not establish the
proper head and length for every received B/C wood hold or the entire Mini
bundle. Record each hold's seat, panel/insert engagement, rear clearance and any
manufacturer-provided pin-screw requirement before ordering. Do not invent
universal hold-bolt lengths or pin-screw quantities.

For **142 × 3 = 426 insert-retaining screws**, select the screws supplied for
the [Escape Standard 3-Hole T-Nut](https://escapeclimbing.com/products/hd3hnut),
or manufacturer-confirmed identical replacements. Its page states screws are
included but supplies no complete screw dimensions: head, diameter, length,
pilot and face-side point cover remain installation inputs. Do not use the
frame's selected R4 screws as replacements by default.

## LEDs and wiring

Reuse the owned MoonBoard lighting, controller and matched factory power supply;
do not buy a guessed replacement harness. The [current kit listing](https://us.moonclimbing.com/products/moonboard-led-kit)
does not override the owner's reported continuous-strip inventory. Record the
received revision, strip/connectors, power-supply label and release method.
Retain removable routing and strain relief, accessible connectors and protection
from sharp steel/fasteners; no new strip cuts, splices or panel-seam connectors.
Exact retainer products and screw lengths follow measured cable and timber
clearances. See [LED installation](v1-led-installation.md); its older offcut
steps are not reinstated here.
