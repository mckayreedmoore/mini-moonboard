This records project choices and calculation scope; it does not assign a climber rating.

## Evidence and open decisions

Geometry and browser checks are complete. The current
[whole-body equilibrium calculation](current-frame-equilibrium.md) addresses
support reactions and overturning, while the
[base-connection basis](current-base-connection-basis.md) records published
catalog directions and their applicability limits. Whole-body equilibrium does
not determine internal frame/joint forces or establish member strength.

The remaining engineering decision is the current frame's internal load sharing,
including rim/header bearing, leg-joint rotation and lateral racking. Once those
forces are justified, check the governing members and connections and issue one
matching cut/drilling/hardware package with independent review. A targeted test
is conditional on a remaining decision-critical uncertainty; it is not a routine
addition to this scope.

Keep dated changes here when a decision changes. Historical rejected designs and
superseded calculations belong in [the archive](history/README.md). Do not reopen
a settled material choice merely because an older report used a different assumption.
