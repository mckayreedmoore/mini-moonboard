# Corner-block stock and common-pattern review

The owner requested common drilling wherever geometry permits, common
dimensional lumber, and a check of a possible three-family layout. The
implemented consolidation uses seven finished designs for 24 blocks. Fifteen
share the same blank and drilling pattern. The other nine retain their current
patterns, including the second vertical bolt in each interior sandwich block.

## Current blanks

Dimensions below are nominal CAD blank requirements before the modeled bores
and reliefs. They are not measured stock or a released cut list. Length follows
the proposed stock grain direction, not the assembled model's world bounding
box; the main member blocks are tilted with the wall.

| Family | Quantity | Blank section × length, mm | Stock route |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Common main-member pattern | 15 | 88.9 × 88.9 × 119.7 | 4×4, crosscut to length |
| Shortened G7 block | 1 | 88.9 × 88.9 × 86.9 | 4×4, shorter crosscut |
| Kicker center-post blocks | 2 | 88.9 × 88.9 × 128.9 | 4×4, crosscut to length |
| Tall central principal/header blocks, handed patterns | 2 | 83.9 × 139.7 × 134.7 | 4×6, trim thickness by 5 mm and crosscut |
| Interior sandwich blocks | 2 | 88.9 × 133.35 × 139.0 | 4×6, trim the 139.7 mm width by 6.35 mm |
| Exterior sandwich spines | 2 | 38.1 × 139.7 × 276.3 | 2×6 blank, retain modeled reliefs |

The ordinary dry dressed sizes used here are 3½ × 3½ inches for 4×4 and
3½ × 5½ inches for 4×6. The inch dimensions are the standard's controlling
units; their exact conversions are 88.9 and 139.7 mm. See
[PS 20-25, Table 3, printed page 15](https://alsc.org/uploaded/PS%2020-25%20Final.pdf#page=15).
This establishes a size convention, not local stock availability or the
condition of delivered lumber.

Three stock sections cover the revised blocks: **18 pieces from 4×4, four
from 4×6 and two from 2×6**. The 2×6 convention is 1½ × 5½ inches
(38.1 × 139.7 mm). Modeled blank lengths total 2,140.2 mm, 547.4 mm and
552.6 mm respectively, before kerfs, end squaring, defects or rejected blanks.
These are planning dimensions, not purchase quantities or released cuts.

The full 88.9 mm and 139.7 mm stock faces have no extra planing allowance in
the model. Rounded mill edges, undersize stock or surfacing loss cannot be
assumed to leave a perfect full rectangular blank. Cutting the interior
sandwich blocks to 133.35 mm removes 6.35 mm from that stock width in total,
including the saw's waste; the kerf is not added to the finished dimension.
Do not replace solid blanks with laminated 2× material. Main-block grain
remains along local N; kicker-post grain remains vertical. A vertical stock
length is the proposed manufacturing orientation for the newer plumb tall
blocks, and still needs to be carried into their later joint checks.

## Historical three-family body trial, not adopted

The owner's proposed grouping is a useful simplification target:

| Proposed family | Quantity | Common blank, mm |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Main member blocks | 16 | 88.9 × 88.9 × 119.7 |
| Exterior sandwich spines | 2 | 88.9 × 139.7 × 269.95, with their existing reliefs |
| Taller blocks | 6 | 88.9 × 139.7 × 139.7 |

A separate body-only trial enlarges G7 away from the panel and enlarges the
four smaller tall blocks while retaining their header seats. The added wood
has no new intersection with the checked existing timber, T-nuts, LEDs,
wires or provisional hold projections. This is encouraging geometry evidence,
not a full three-design layout. It does not check new hardware occupancy,
insertion paths or hole placement in those enlarged blanks.

The six tall blocks all have two transverse and two vertical bolts after the
requested extra sandwich bolts, but their axes occupy different stations.
Their modeled bore diameters also differ: 7.3 mm at the center nodes and
7.5 mm at the outer sandwiches. Those are CAD envelopes, not drill-bit
instructions. The handed central patterns are not interchangeable by a
proper rotation in the current layout. Making all six interchangeable would
require a further receiver/hardware layout change, rather than simply naming
them one part. The owner subsequently clarified to keep common patterns
where they fit and retain necessary exceptions, so this three-family body
trial is not adopted.

retains the separate body trial. No strength,
edge/end-distance resistance, installation sequence or fabrication release is
established by this stock review. Joint evaluations remain paused.
