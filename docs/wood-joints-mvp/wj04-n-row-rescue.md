## Tool paths and remaining allowance

The upper rail spans every tested N. Moving the row does not change the tool's T-direction gap. The [FACOM RB.7/16](https://www.facom.com/product/rb716/716-drive-12-point-thin-wall-socket?tid=609036) 22 mm exterior plus the [GEARWRENCH 81011T](https://www.gearwrench.com/all-tools/ratchets-sockets/ratchets-drive-tools/81011t-14-drive-90-tooth-teardrop-ratchet-5) listed 8.89 mm head thickness leaves **0.0554 mm nominal** before the upper rail throughout the nut-unthread stroke. The socket alone leaves 8.9454 mm. This has no real tolerance allowance for driver tang, socket square-drive connection, bolt length and washer variation, stock/cut error, deflection, or hand motion. The socket's internal stud-through depth remains unpublished; the seated bolt projects 11.9126–16.256 mm beyond its nut. A zero-clash envelope with this nominal 0.0554 mm gap cannot establish usable ratchet access.

A documented open-end alternative avoids the socket's long axial extension: the [FACOM 22.11/32×7/16 midget wrench catalog](https://www.cheefatt.com/media/wysiwyg/Catalogue/Facom/Idemco-Adjustable_Wrenches_Catalogue.pdf) lists a 7/16 jaw, **3 mm head thickness, 23 mm head width, 100 mm overall length**, with the heads set at 15°. Its 3 mm head is 2.3848 mm thinner than the minimum listed nut height. A 23 mm width-only cylindrical head bound translates along the nut path without a modeled solid hit and leaves **26.753 mm** to the upper rail at the end; the detached nut leaves **25.205 mm**. This tests axial access only. It does not include the actual open jaw, handle, or rotation. For scale, a 100 mm radius handle moved through 30° or 60° traces 52.36 or 104.72 mm of arc, respectively. The actual required regrip angle, orientation, handle sweep, and force access have not been established from the catalog data or screened against protected services. The 100 mm wrench length is an upper bound on pivot-to-tip radius, not an adopted operating stroke.

The paired rail axes stay 25.4 mm apart in X. Their maximum 24.892 mm ratchet-head width bounds have 0.508 mm nominal separation if held side by side; this is a simultaneous-head comparison, not an assembly tolerance. The nearest fixed principal-bolt N center remains at least 28 mm from the rail row; even a 12.446 mm ratchet-head radius plus a 9.5123 mm maximum product-washer radius leaves 6.0417 mm N separation. This checks installed principal hardware by an axis bound, not its service tool. The detached-nut route screened only the stated **100 mm rearward move after axial unthreading**; its temporary excess past the ordinary N limit is shown above. A service/assembly instruction would have been required if this parked spacer route were selected; it is not part of the current WJ-04 configuration.

## Disposition

There is **no accepted N offset**. N = 257.5–265 mm improves the conditional cleat 4D end coordinate without nominal solid clashes, but it leaves the fixed rail X-end and principal T-edge questions. The socket/ratchet stack has only 0.0554 mm nominal rail gap and no proved internal stud/driver interface; the thin open-end wrench has a plausible axial route but no proved handle swing. WJ-03 integration, product receiving fit, washer/sleeve bearing, full joint mechanics, and stock/cut tolerances remain open. No strength, fabrication, or release claim follows from this sweep.
