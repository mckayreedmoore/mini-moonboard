## Distinguish geometric support from mechanical support

WJ24 geometry can establish whether each fixed axis intersects the named finished receiver and whether a proposed support surface exists. With exact finished solids it can also measure receiver entry intervals, distances to edges/ends/cuts/other holes, minimum remaining wood ligaments, countersink and panel-hole intersections, candidate panel-to-frame contact footprints, and continuous edge-overlap lengths. These are geometry results, not capacities.

For the two center backers, the four fixed kicker screws reach nominal backer receivers. The existing inner-edge screen samples three points per backer edge; it does not establish continuous bearing width, active contact, pressure, tolerance reserve, or load transfer. The separate [backer load-path contract](wj24-backer-loadpath-contract.md) follows two provisional through-bolts per backer into the base header, then through distinct header-to-center-post and header-to-principal-cleat joints. None of those connections is accepted. A center screw entering a backer is not a completed frame load path.

Geometry may calculate the potential panel/wood bearing area and the edge support available after cuts. It cannot determine which parts of those faces are active under a case, whether a face opens, the pressure distribution, or how much load goes to each screw. A stress formed as force divided by nominal area is not a bearing check until the case reaction, active area, panel/wood material values, grain, and applicable method are established. Do not credit friction, preload, numerical face bonding, or equal sharing without a frozen, evidence-backed model.

| Geometry may establish | Geometry alone cannot establish |
| --- | --- |
| Exact fixed-axis identity and direct/redirected WJ24 receiver mapping. | Hillman withdrawal, lateral, bending, pull-through, combined resistance, or stiffness. |
| Finished host material at the axis, measured boundary distances, remaining ligaments, and cut/edge overlap. | Whether a nominal ligament resists splitting, breakout, tear-out, bearing, or local panel failure. |
| Potential plywood-to-timber faces, their normals, overlap polygons, and unsupported spans. | Active contact, opening, pressure, force transfer, contact stiffness, or an equilibrium path. |
| A nominal penetration interval from specified dimensions and a measured screw profile. | Thread length engaged in the receiving member, effective withdrawal length, or a capacity from overall screw length. |
| Spatial relation of the four center screws and two backers to the kerf-right kicker. | Continuous kicker-edge support or transfer from kicker to backer/header/frame from a few point samples. |

The face plywood's purchase record identifies Roseburg AC fir, 23/32 CAT, PS1-09 Exterior, but does not identify the delivered sheet's ply count, strength axis/grade, or design values. That source supports geometry and product identity only until its strength basis is established. A category or actual thickness alone is not a panel strength value; see the [purchased plywood record](../purchased-materials.md).

The candidate structural basis is the project's frozen 2024 NDS route, not a generic deck-screw extrapolation. AWC identifies wood-screw connection provisions in the 2024 NDS. The AWC Chapter 12 commentary for the prior edition explains that ANSI/ASME B18.6.1 supplies quality and dimensional requirements but does not assign the screw's metal strength properties; WJ-08 must bind the exact 2024 clause and errata, not transfer a prior-edition value. Accordingly, a potential NDS calculation must establish product conformity and supply the needed steel properties, exact penetration, actual receiving wood properties, installation, and all applicable factors. The checks must include applicable single-screw withdrawal and steel-root tension, head pull-through on the plywood side member, lateral yield in each relevant direction/shear plane, combined lateral-plus-withdrawal interaction, and spacing/edge/end/splitting requirements. Use the current 2024 provisions, supplement, errata, and the project's selected design format; keep a missing or inapplicable method open. The [AWC Connection Calculator description](https://awc.org/resources/connection-calculator/) also makes clear that its stated scope is single-fastener capacities; it does not produce this candidate's group distribution or complete frame action.

Small-screw edge and group behavior needs its own applicability decision. The measured screw diameter, not its nominal #10 label, controls. The prior NDS commentary notes that the tabulated detailing rules do not specify all placement values for wood screws under 1/4 in and offers recommended distances rather than a screw-group capacity. WJ-08 must verify the current 2024 text and any applicable method for these axes. If the exact small-screw edge/splitting condition remains uncovered, retain it as a specific method gap or use a validated exact-layout test; do not infer a pass from the nominal axis-to-edge measurement or from a recommended-spacing table alone.

Where the exact screw or plywood basis does not fit the adopted calculation method, product-specific evidence is needed. ASTM D1761-20(2025) provides a basic procedure for evaluating withdrawal and lateral resistance of fasteners in wood and wood-based materials. That single-fastener test method does not by itself establish a 66-screw group response, head pull-through for this countersunk face, a combined-action envelope, a design adjustment, or frame load sharing. A project-specific test route must predefine a defensible statistical/design basis and include the exact 42605 product, actual DF-L and Roseburg panel lots or bounded equivalent properties, receiver grain, actual pilot/countersink/edge distances/penetration, directional single-screw response, head pull-through, and representative screw-group layouts. It must also establish force-slip stiffness and group load distribution or test the complete panel/backer assembly under the relevant combined actions. Do not convert a mean test load or an offcut result directly into an allowable.

## Bounded path to MVP-E without a general Hillman qualification claim

There are two defensible paths; neither treats the brand or #10 label as a capacity.

1. **Show the 66 screws are not needed for the structural load path.** In WJ-08/WJ-09, remove screw force and stiffness from the analysis while retaining their fixed locations in the physical inventory. Solve all six recorded cases with the actual unilateral panel-to-frame contacts and no unsubstantiated friction, preload, glue, or numerical tie. Demonstrate equilibrium, no panel/kicker separation or unstable sliding/rotation, and sufficient independent edge support and retention under every applicable use/support direction. Only then may the screws be identified as installed panel-retention hardware that is not relied on for structural transfer; document the exact zero-screw sensitivity and its scope. If that model separates or cannot close the applied loads, this route is disproved. A missing reaction is not zero demand.
2. **Evaluate the exact assembly under an applicable method.** If a screw carries load, first establish a valid NDS wood-screw basis and all metal, wood, plywood, geometry, interaction and load-sharing inputs. If that is not possible from exact published/product data, use a bounded, project-specific test program with a stated design/statistical basis and WJ24-matched specimens/group response. The outcome may support only the defined WJ24 connection under its recorded assumptions; it must not be reported as a general Hillman 42605 qualification or transferred to another installation. No blanket outside sign-off is introduced; only the specific missing product or joint evidence must be supplied.

The current evidence does not show that path 1 passes, and it does not provide the inputs for path 2. Until one closes, the Hillman-dependent receiver and panel load path remain unresolved. Therefore the 66 axes may remain in the conditional geometry/shop package, but they cannot be marked mechanically accepted in WJ-08/09, and the WJ-10 MVP-E endpoint cannot describe the required load path as resolved. Keep actual receiving and installation observations blank until the later physical WJ-11 work; this contract authorizes no drilling, fabrication, or climbing use.
