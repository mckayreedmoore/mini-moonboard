# Revised wood-joint hardware budget

This pricing checkpoint predates the [2×6 exterior-block revision](hypotheses/led-clearance-2x6-runner-blocks-2026-09-24/README.md). Four modeled 6-in bolts are now 4-in envelopes and four 11.5-in envelopes are now 9.5-in. Counts are unchanged, but the inventory and prices below describe the earlier checkpoint. The bottom-center tail clashes mentioned below have since been cleared by block trimming. No updated product selection or price quote is implied.

Allow **$150–200 USD for the structural bolts, nuts and washers**, before tax
and shipping. The detailed allowance is **$130.64–178.00**; the rounded budget
leaves room for small quantity and price differences. This covers the revised
24-block model with two vertical bolts in each interior sandwich block: 92
candidate bolt assemblies plus the twelve starting frame assemblies. It
excludes the already purchased 66 Hillman panel/kicker screws from new spending.

This is a September 24, 2026 planning estimate, not a priced purchase schedule.
The four long sandwich bolts are the largest sourcing uncertainty; an exact
carbon-steel product matching their required shank has not been priced. The
range is an allowance, not a guaranteed ceiling. Final length, thread transition,
washer dimensions, nut engagement and material selection remain unresolved.
No joint evaluation or hardware substitution is made by this budget.

## Already purchased and excluded

The [purchase record](../current-panel-screw-purchase.md) identifies the Hillman
42605 screws for all 66 panel/kicker locations. Their receipt cost is not
recorded, so neither historic spending nor a replacement value is invented.
They contribute $0 additional purchase allowance here. The 24 former ML24Z
angles and 144 SDS screws are not included in this wood-block development
candidate's hardware cost.

This estimate excludes timber and block blanks, tools and drill bits,
consumables, tax and freight. Holds, hold bolts, T-nuts, T-nut retention screws,
anti-rotation screws and LED hardware are a separate climbing-surface inventory;
missing quantities and owner stock are not reconciled here. It is therefore a
structural-frame hardware estimate, not a complete MoonBoard acquisition cost.
