# Bounded online connection-method applicability check

The owner priority remains the BG001/BG003/BG045 corner assembly. This
lookup does not qualify the floor, reopen original leg/runner resistance,
or change the selected geometry or native methods.

[Swedish Wood, Design of Timber Structures, Volume 2 (2022)](https://www.swedishwood.com/siteassets/5-publikationer/pdfer/sw-design-of-timber-structures-vol2-2022.pdf)
was inspected on September 30, 2026. Page 33, Table 9.2 gives serviceability
slip modulus per shear plane and fastener and explicitly treats bolt
clearance separately from slip. This supports keeping the zero-gap scenario
conditional; a stiffness proxy does not resolve actual clearance engagement.
Pages 36–37 give single- and double-shear yield modes and point to Volume 1
section 4.9.3 and EN 1995-1-1 Annex A for block/plug shear. These are separate
mechanisms from fastener yielding. The displayed double-shear figure uses a
single central member with the same outer-member thickness symbol. It does
not establish a resistance rule for the observed BG003 unequal,
non-collinear outer actions. No Eurocode characteristic resistance, factor,
or material class is transferred to the existing NDS reference calculations.

[AWC, 2018 Manual for Engineered Wood Construction](https://web-media.awc.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/17210413/AWC-2018-Manual-1810.pdf)
identifies NDS Appendix E as the local-stress evaluation route for fastener
groups. The current local-wood screen already distinguishes net-section,
row/group shear and perpendicular-grain splitting applicability. This
lookup is therefore a source cross-check, not a reason to repeat that
screen or assume its topology limits have disappeared.

The next engineering result is a source-supported asymmetric BG003 method
or necessary bounds, with its exact assumptions. In parallel, remaining
case-specific support compatibility must pass before those corner actions
can enter an envelope. No complete-joint acceptance is established here.
