# BG001 Appendix E parallel-row screen

Status: **conditional component screen only**. This result does not accept or
reject BG001, qualify the joint, or release fabrication or climbing use. It
answers one method question: Appendix E supports checking a fastener-group
loaded-end row and a candidate net section from the signed group load; it does
not require a continuum force resultant recovered on the hole-center cut for
those Appendix E calculations.

The reproducible screen is [`screen.json`](screen.json), generated by
[`produce.py`](produce.py). From the repository root, run:

```sh
python3 docs/wood-joints-mvp/hypotheses/mvp-acceleration-2026-09-28/current-bg001-appendix-e-parallel-row-screen-attempt01/produce.py
```

The script rejects changed source hashes, candidate/case identity, failed
response gates, changed BG001 axes, and changed row geometry. It uses the
existing `dfl_parallel_row_tear_out_reference_lbf` and
`dfl_net_parallel_tension_reference_lbf` methods in
[`bolted_timber_checks.py`](../../../../../mini_moonboard/bolted_timber_checks.py)
without editing them.

## Method finding

NDS 2024 Appendix E.1 describes a single fastener or closely spaced group
loaded parallel to grain, for which local wood failure may be net section or
fastener-row tear-out. E.2 gives `Z′NT = F′t A_net`. E.3 gives row tear-out;
under E.3.1's two-shear-line assumption, the one-row expression is
`Z′RT = n F′v t s_critical`, with `s_critical` equal to the lesser of loaded-end
distance and row pitch. The E.6 example compares these local wood resistances
to the connection-group lateral load. Therefore an Appendix E group screen
uses the signed group action and code-defined geometry; it does not need an
FEA traction/free-body force at the hole-center cut. The hole-center net plane
is a candidate E.2 capacity section, while the loaded-end row is a separate
E.3 path. Neither is established as the unique physical failure path here.

The actual full BG001 action does not meet a demonstrated pure-parallel-only
condition. In the a12-rear full-factor increment, the two modeled lateral-plane
forces on the spine are approximately `[-151.25, -261.00] N` and
`[+213.22, -258.46] N` in global Y/Z. Their group sum is
`[+61.97, -519.46] N` in Y/Z, but the individual bolt-plane actions are oblique
to the proposed +Z grain at about 30° and 40°. The two separate outer-seat ties
also add axial force along X. The table reports the +Z parallel component as a
conditional directional screen and keeps the cross-grain and axial parts
separate; it does not invent a mixed-action Appendix E interaction or claim a
conservative combined ratio. Thus this method finding removes a blanket need
for bore-resolved force recovery to calculate an Appendix E reference, but it
does not remove the mixed-action, splitting, tie, or complete-member checks.

## Conditional arithmetic

The selected source is the audited `a12-rear` case at time 1.0/load factor
1.0. The response report's MPC, nonlinear spring, retained-bilateral,
selected-floor, inactive-floor, body-balance, and global-balance gates all pass
for that one conditional native response. The bound input model carries the
proposed +Z grain vectors for the spine and post. The report itself says the
joint is not accepted, and its forces do not qualify other cases.

The row consists of two bolts with 42.05 mm pitch through members 38.1 mm thick
along the bolt axis. The signed lateral group action points toward the spine's
negative-Z end and the post's positive-Z end. Their loaded-end distances are
31.75 mm and 25.4 mm respectively, both below the pitch. The resulting
`s_critical` values are 31.75 mm for the spine and 25.4 mm for the post. The
parallel component magnitude is 519.4605 N (116.7794 lbf) on each member.

Using the existing helper's unadjusted dry DF-L No. 2 base value `Fv = 180 psi`,
the row references are 675 lbf (3002.55 N) for the spine and 540 lbf (2402.04 N)
for the post. Ratios to these unadjusted base references are about 0.173 and
0.216. They are reference-only component ratios, not adjusted-capacity ratios
or design DCRs. The applicable solid-sawn `F′v` factors under NDS Chapter 4
§4.3 remain pending until the material and service inputs are established.
The earlier `Cvr = 0.72` sensitivity has been removed: the March 2026 errata's
§5.3.10 is in the glulam chapter and addresses `Fvx`/`Fvy`; it is not the
solid-sawn adjustment rule. Chapter 4 §4.3/Table 4.3.1 governs sawn-lumber
adjustments, and no source extending the Chapter 5 Cvr factor to these members
is bound here.

For the spine, the pinned geometry screen gives one candidate XY net section
through a modeled 7.5 mm X-bore, with area 5036.82 mm². Applying the same
parallel component to the helper's unadjusted `Ft = 575 psi` gives a 4489.07 lbf
(19.968 kN) net-tension reference and a base-reference component ratio of
0.0260. This is not a recovered section force or a final adjusted capacity;
actual cuts, bore size, material, and load applicability remain unverified.

The row reference is lower than the spine's unadjusted E.2 net-section
reference in this scenario, but that comparison does not establish a governing
limit state. The simultaneous cross-grain bolt forces, separate X-axis seat
ties, connections at BG003/BG045, member section actions, group interactions,
and other failure modes remain outside these ratios. Do not sum capacities or
transfer the BG001 row result to BG003, BG045, or the retained LEG/RUNNER bolts.

## Source binding and limits

`screen.json` records exact SHA-256 pins for the demand report, source model,
response audit, section geometry screen, helper, project method basis, and
NDS-source index. The 2024 Appendix PDF source pin is SHA-256
`99b0a54e7133b3cf6dd156d8fe864c1717c951487bf5baa99c34da9a68a6bbb7`; the
pinned copy was checked at printed pages 174–175 (PDF pages 9–10). The official
AWC source is the [2024 NDS Appendix PDF](https://web-media.awc.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/17210019/AWC_NDS2024_withCommentary_20240719_AWCWebsite_Appendix.pdf).
The [March 2026 AWC 2024 NDS errata](https://awc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2024-NDS-Errata-and-Addenda-03.23.26.pdf)
was checked for Cvr scope. Its §5.3.10 provision is in Chapter 5 and refers to
`Fvx`/`Fvy` for structural glued-laminated timber; this screen does not transfer
that factor to the solid-sawn BG001 members. Their adjusted `F′v` remains
subject to Chapter 4 §4.3 and Table 4.3.1. No `Cvr` factor is applied here.
The pinned errata PDF SHA-256 is
`b3f4f8b3b2e2ffa5eb618de9e1182614c329d9e4a135096667364dc9a8e473c0`.
The [AWC 2024 NDS page](https://awc.org/resources/2024-nds/) identifies the
edition as the ANSI-approved 2024 standard.

Not evaluated here: final adjusted wood values; stock/species/grade/moisture;
as-built bore/cut geometry; full mixed parallel/cross-grain/axial interaction;
cross-grain splitting; bolt bending/yield and axial interaction; washer bearing;
member internal axial/bending/shear actions; complete BG001/BG003/BG045 path;
the other five demand cases; required engagement and material/floor sensitivities.
