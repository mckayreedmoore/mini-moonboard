# Thin bolted panel transfer method

The reviewed thin model has a fresh flexible-panel method and completed fixed-receiver controls. It does **not** have accepted panel or screw resistance. These controls supply method evidence for the coupled frame assessment; their forces cannot replace a compatible frame/panel solution. They retain all 66 Hillman 42605 axes, the six original signed climber cases, 250 lb × 2 downward force, 300 N horizontal force, 100 mm hold lever and the original 25 kg accessory distribution. No native solver, geometry change, additional screw or physical operation occurred.

The [assessment](panel-mechanics-v4.json) retains 126 panel responses: six panels × six live cases plus permanent load × three stiffness scenarios. It also retains all 462 individual screw actions and receiver compression wrenches for the 1,000 N/mm scenario. The [validation receipt](panel-mechanics-validation-v4.json) binds the producer, tests, exact geometry cache, reference sources, saved controls and their executed producer snapshots. The roughly 954 kB assessment is permanent because it preserves simultaneous screw demands, equilibrium residuals, net-section cuts and each edge-region disposition. Matrix arrays and intermediate runs remain in ignored `fea/generated/thin-bolted-panel/`; they are not another CAD export or historical frame solve.

The method uses a cubic tensor B-spline Ritz energy with membrane displacement and outward plate bending. Its six floating rigid motions are retained. Exact cached finished faces determine compression backing, including the proposed bores and shallow front-open wire channels. Clipped triangle integration is coalesced by receiver into area- and centroid-preserving contact cells. Wood support carries compression only; screw heads carry tension only. Support friction, preload and initial gaps are zero scenarios. The lateral bearing point is at the panel midplane. The axial head displacement averages the conditional 9 mm/5 mm annulus. Every frame/panel port retains its Kirchhoff thickness arm; the hold load vector includes the 100 mm lever exactly once.

The generic circular-head reference reuses the authenticated NDS scalar helper and APA Group 1 head-pull-through ESG **0.50**. Applicability to Hillman, actual thread engagement excluding its tip, root/yield strength, wood grain/detail and combined axial/lateral interaction remain unqualified.
