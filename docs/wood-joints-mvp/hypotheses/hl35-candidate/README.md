## Catalog basis and limits

**HL35** is the manufacturer model name; the owner's “HL35s” is interpreted as
the plural. A distinct “HL35S” Simpson SKU has not been verified.
The readable manufacturer-authored
[C-C-2021 HL page, page 292](https://device.report/m/566c4ff6a72fec5f888f79af5915ebe858e6abbfae5c10476d7cc776ab45e1b9.pdf)
gives 3¼-inch flange reaches, 5-inch bend length, four ½-inch bolts,
3½-inch minimum receiving wood and 2½-inch hole pitch. It also requires
centered bearing on a sufficiently wide member and opposing connectors for
lateral loads in both directions. Its tabulated 740 lb uplift and 1,310 lb F1
values are at the stated 160% duration basis. These are reference entries,
**not adopted climbing capacities**.

The manufacturer [current catalog landing page](https://www.strongtie.com/resources/literature/wood-construction-connectors-catalog?source=topnav)
identifies C-C-2026. An available
[manufacturer-authored current HL page](https://dhcsupplies.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/Documents/simpson-strong-tie/hl-angles.pdf)
is hosted by a supplier. Its exact current page and factory CAD must be
authenticated and read before adopting a product-specific resistance table.
The older readable page identifies 8-gauge HL35, while retailer descriptions
and historical repository envelopes use 7 gauge. This discrepancy is unresolved.
The model consequently uses an explicit **5 mm planning occupancy envelope**,
with no inferred steel yield strength or delivered thickness.

The horizontal flange hole offset is not fully dimensioned in the cited
drawing; **50.8 mm is a provisional symmetric coordinate**, inherited as an
explicit assumption from the earlier HL35 fit study. Bend radius, factory
hole dimensions, coating and tolerances are unmeasured. No custom steel
fabrication is proposed.

Two connectors may allow the cited uplift treatment when the complete
installation is applicable. **Their lateral rating may not be doubled.**
Rotating an angle with a sloped member also rotates its catalog force axes;
global horizontal or vertical force cannot simply be assigned to F1/uplift.
The HL35 table does not supply an arbitrary transverse or six-component moment
capacity. A complete joint must establish force and moment transfer through
the actual bolt group, bearing/contact and receivers, without inventing those
missing capacities or treating each angle as an independent duplicate joint.

not disappear by changing the structural connector. No native solve is ready
or performed for this candidate. No-slip floor support remains unverified.
