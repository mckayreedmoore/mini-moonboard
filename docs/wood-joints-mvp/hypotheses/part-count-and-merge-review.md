## Bounded merge options

- **WJ-03 bridge and under-header link:** merge each same-side bridge/link pair
  into one stepped member. Across both sides, this saves two pieces and four
  bolt positions, reducing the projection to 26 pieces and 100 positions.
  Geometry needs a shaped 4×6 blank, a long rip and shoulder cut. Final-section
  grade evidence and a safe supported cutting method are not established.
  Keep the spine separate; its grain direction differs.
- **Center C-saddle:** a per-side saddle could replace separate upper/lower
  cleats in principle, but its approximate 208.9 × 162.7 × 229.8 mm envelope
  needs undocumented stock with at least 208.9 × 162.7 mm actual section and
  229.8 mm length for the proposed vertical grain. It changes the upper
  cleat’s grain direction, wraps the header, and requires accessible bolt
  tools. No matching stock or complete joint path is established. Current
  center-body geometry clears the backers; the separate tool screen has
  sector-specific ratchet/backer hits and remaining post hardware/timber hits.
  Those are open access checks, not proof the layouts cannot coexist.
- **Rail straps:** one strap across each lower/upper pair is only a layout
  hypothesis. It adds 38.1 mm rearward projection beyond the recorded cap and
  changes the bolt face and access direction. Flush housing is outside current
  scope; the opposite face reaches the panel side. A provisional six-bolt
  strap pattern might save one piece and two bolt positions per pair, but cannot
  replace the current eight positions without new load, edge-distance,
  service, insertion, and access checks. Reusing this across four pairs would
  at best reduce the projection to 24 pieces and 96 positions, before other
  merges.

Even the quantified bridge/link merge leaves 26 pieces. Do not adopt shaped,
ripped, C-saddle, or over-depth alternatives solely to reduce counts. Preserve
current constraints and require source-bound geometry, stock, access, and
complete-joint evidence before selecting any merge.
