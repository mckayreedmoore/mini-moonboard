## Interface and energy method

The [frame replacement API](../joint-frame-member-replacement.py) subtracts
the selected old body's complete H/e/L contribution once and adds the actual
bored or cracked body's contribution once. It retains the surrounding timber,
continuous shafts, washers and original connector laws. The selected body uses
the original global rigid datum and full current port order. No preload,
friction, reinforcement or invented cross-crack stitch is credited.

Original native B and F supply the complete force and **all nine first moments**
of each unit right-hand side. These targets distinguish affine strain work
that a six-component wrench alone cannot determine. For example, an axial
action line point outside the cleat is not an actual timber-seat location.
Contact and outer-seat footprints additionally require nonnegative scalar
normal traction. A target outside the retained physical patch or requiring
adhesion stops that mapping; it is not silently repaired by arbitrary signed
contact loads. Bilateral bolt traction and consistent external body-load
footprints have separate declared hypotheses.

The proved localized union/Schur helpers recover each original full field.
Only rigid pose is removed while forming the elastic inverse. The native
operator audit retains full H/e/L reciprocity, gravity/live cross terms,
original-node/stiffness identities, source rigid work and all-node equilibrium.
Full null spectra remain unassessed; topology alone does not prove them.

After global redistribution, the comparison is
`G = (Pi_initial - Pi_final) / added_sound_area`. Each Pi record includes full
assembled compliance and connector energy, rigid external work and the selected
body's complete twelve-by-twelve load-energy matrix L. Unchanged bodies' load
constants cancel only under the same source frame, external coefficient vector
and frozen constant token. The token names the authenticated current eligible
baseline receipt; the original native frame remains a separate source binding.
The unchanged additive constant remains unknown. The resulting records do not claim an absolute
assembly potential. The earlier fixed-force `U_final - U_initial` calculation
and its contact upper bound are not transferred. A new upper comparison needs
its own assembled conservative-bound proof.

The 0.5 MPa transverse strength and 0.1 N/mm common mixed-mode fracture
threshold remain explicit, unmeasured conditional hypotheses. Stress peaks
require their own mesh assessment. Reference exceedances are numerical outcomes,
not observations of timber splitting or fabrication/climbing acceptance.
