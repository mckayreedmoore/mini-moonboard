# Prescribed current-force shaft recovery

This adapter applies authenticated scalar bolt axial and signed two-component
lateral forces to the existing two-host, first-order beam and contact helper.
It supplies isolated shaft fields and signed own-end moments where the helper's
unchanged equilibrium gates pass. It does not replace scalar joint laws, refresh
frame forces, or establish globally compatible shaft curvature. Reference
exceedances remain recorded outcomes.

The source joins preserve the actual accepted case/gap subset and leave excluded
states unavailable. Preparation verifies all 100 scalar geometries against the
finished feature register, original bore/spans and source interface. The existing
16 matched numerical endpoint normalizations retain their frozen proof and
tolerance. No geometry query or change is performed. Four continuous knee shafts
remain a separate compatible source: the original 12-state packet has 96 own
ends; the new action packet requires its own eight ends per accepted state.

The working washer contract selects source-diameter profiles. It includes the
eight wider top-rail seats, retains the 10-mm restricted shaft-contact diameter
at both ends of `center_principal_right_2`, and keeps four top-side source-quarter
versus shop-5/16 mismatches explicit. A larger actual plate is not reduced to the
restricted ring. Actual plate stresses and support masks are a separate method.

The initial action03 pilot is method evidence under the original frame08 forces.
The final all100 sweep requires a preparation explicitly labelled
`washer_updated_compatible_frame` and bound to its new action receipt. The four
updated top-rail axial seat stiffnesses require new frame forces; old forces do
not become a result of that update.

The bounded pilot chooses maximum lateral force, maximum tension, minimum
strictly positive tension and maximum zero-effective-gap lateral force in eight
engineering categories. These categories are test coverage, not the completion
plan's formal connection-family inventory. Source tension is never rounded to
zero. Nominal and zero effective bore clearance use the source gap scale. The
drive follows the signed physical source lateral vector for every nonzero force.

The exact finite spring energy supplies a Clarabel initial pose. At exactly zero
tension, the existing rank-aware convex-hinge initializer supplies a stationary
pose without artificial stiffness. The original beam, 48 bore samples, two
series annular contacts, 150 Newton steps, 50 Armijo backtracks, 1e-6 N stationary
and physical force gates, and length-scaled moment gate remain authoritative.
Recovered fields contain 80 same-state positions. The steel comparison retains
simultaneous axial/bending/shear at each position and the existing separate
nominal thread/average-shear references; peaks from different positions are not
combined into an invented section demand.

`washer-ends.jsonl` uses schema `joint_frame_prescribed_shaft_own_end/v1` and
join key `[force_branch, state_tag, axis_id, end_role, receiver_member]`. It saves
signed axial force, normal compression, world force/moment, physical transverse
basis, seat datum, normal into the receiver, rigid contact state and independent
wood/hardware pressure wrenches. A complete end requires the existing 1e-7 N and
1e-6 Nmm pressure wrench gates. These saved rigid pressures are initialization
data for an actual plate model, not actual washer metal stress or supported-mask
qualification. Unsupported moments stay unknown.

Import and preparation are inert with respect to project solves. `--mode known`
checks a cantilever, concentric annular compression including positive tensions
down to 1e-18 N, and the existing zero-tension null/gap analytic oracle. Parent
owns `--mode pilot` and `--mode all` project execution. Every fresh ignored child
saves exact producer/document snapshots, source/output hashes, recovered fields,
end records and numerical traces. Existing frozen producers and receipts stay
unchanged.

The four top-side source lateral points do not coincide with their finished
shaft interfaces: the transverse offsets are 42.875 mm for side_1 and 8.025 mm
for side_2, on both sides. Preparation retains the exact point forces, source
tie points and outer-seat points, and calculates each force-equivalent couple
about the physical interface. The planar ordinary helper cannot absorb those
couples silently. The original action03 packet's 48 axis states and 96 own ends
remain explicit source/geometry method limits;
they are not numerical field passes. Existing corner redistributed forces do
not supply an applicable current per-axis solution.

Every record contains lateral, axial-tie and total parallel/perpendicular couple

the old side-pair model's redistributed fields. The washer-updated force branch
must receive its own exact action join and couple census.
