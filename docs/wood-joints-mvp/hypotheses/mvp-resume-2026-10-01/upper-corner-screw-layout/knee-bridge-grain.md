actions directly. Original-cut replay errors remain below 0.000001 N/N·mm.

Comparison geometry is the actual rectangular blank minus four preserved
`u` bores and two proposed 7.5-mm-envelope `v` bores. At each new center the
net area is 80.315% of the original section. The envelope is not a drill size.
The common longitudinal-strain, rectangular-region transverse-force sharing,
equal-modulus twist and end-bridge continuity hypotheses remain the original
nominal section method. Continuous-station maxima, hole stress concentrations,
splitting and elastic compatibility are not established by these comparisons.

The [normal-transfer proposal](knee-spine-reinforcement.md) uses equal,
concentric axial end-force pairs. Under that declared symmetry, the added
bolt/end-bearing pairs have zero whole-body and grain-cut wrench. This identity
does not solve their elastic allocation. Original gravity remains fixed for
this proposal comparison; removed wood and added hardware require the later
adopted model's mass reconciliation.
