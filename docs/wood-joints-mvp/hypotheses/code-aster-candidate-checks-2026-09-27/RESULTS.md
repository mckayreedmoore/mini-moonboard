# Candidate-specific Code_Aster method results

Stock Code_Aster passes the actual first A09 bolt/nut map inertia check, including its zero-density carrier. A source-ordered audit reconciles the curved-contact gap output with the correct contact quadrature samples and passes the frozen gap/penetration screens. A body-restricted `FORC_NODA` route now recovers cut-balanced global force and first-moment resultants on the curved crop, invariant under the 95-slave-cell order reversal; the reported nodal `RN` remains inconsistent and order-sensitive. This stress-derived route does not establish local pressure. A flat-coupon-calibrated full-face `LAGS_C` integral gives close empirical closure on the curved crop, but source review shows it is not a reconstruction of the solver residual. `CALC_PRESSION` fails in the pinned image; direct stress extraction passes only a 2-D plane known-answer. Full-joint translation remains deferred pending a validated local contact-pressure/residual route. This is method evidence, not joint resistance or candidate acceptance.

All runs used unmodified Code_Aster 17.4.0 in `simvia/code_aster@sha256:d8d19ea91989eac0d38195bc5795c54c69f530f7196f53d67697ffa57c9106d5`. Luna agents at maximum reasoning effort prepared and audited separate fixtures; the parent froze inputs, serialized native execution and checked provenance. The reviewed development geometry and selected baseline were preserved.

## Actual map and carrier

The [three-case audit](actual-map-audit.json) passes all eleven saved states for direct loading, the literal six-row weighted nut map, and the same map with a zero-density carrier. Each case uses the same physical nodal load and the actual first bolt mesh: 11,348 physical nodes and 5,490 quadratic tetrahedra. The carrier adds 1,107 nodes and 519 tetrahedra. Every source equation contains 754 terms; source coordinates, connectivity and equation coefficient text were checked exactly.

Displacement, velocity, acceleration, measured control-to-carrier motion and independently recomputed physical kinetic energy all pass the [frozen limits](actual-map-readiness.json). The largest physical field error is 4.97e-8 times its allowed bound; the largest pairwise field difference is 4.82e-8 times its bound. Maximum relative kinetic-energy error is 3.39e-13 against a 1e-5 limit. This establishes one infinitesimal global-Y motion mode for one actual map and carrier, without contact, preload, finite rotation, timber resistance or thread behavior.

The energy reference uses the pinned solver's FPG15 curved TETRA10 mass integration. Before using it, an independent [30-by-30 native mass-matrix check](mass-probe-attempt01/parent-audit.json) compared all 900 entries; maximum error normalized by the largest expected entry was 1.26e-15. A four-point rule differs by 22.15% in matrix Frobenius norm on that deliberately curved fixture, so merely matching total mass would not have been sufficient.

The first direct attempt exhausted its CPU allowance while writing results. A resource-only repeat increased that allowance; successful direct, mapped and carrier runs took approximately 168, 182 and 266 seconds. The mapped runs each emit two `DISCRETS_26` warnings that the point controls have no assigned mass and therefore use zero mass. That matches the fixture's intended massless controls; the physical mass and energy checks remain independent. MED coordinate serialization differs from source text by at most 1.14e-13 mm; source node-ID groups resolve identity, with a separate metadata tolerance of 1e-10 mm.

## Curved contact

The contact fixture retains actual pair-021 geometry: 95 slave and 88 master quadratic triangular faces, 183 adjacent quadratic tetrahedra and 658 nodes. It uses generic isotropic elastic properties solely for a method check. All 218 slave contact nodes have an independent curved-surface gap reference in separated states. The cycle includes opening, approach, compression, unloading, reopening and reclosure.

Attempt 01 stopped reading the mesh because connectivity lines exceeded the ASTER reader's 80-column limit. Attempt 02 wrapped those lines without changing any whitespace-separated token, then stopped in the default geometry fixed-point iteration at zero imposed motion. Attempt 03 changed only `ALGO_RESO_GEOM` to `NEWTON`, a documented continuous-contact option that updates pairing during Newton iterations. The [method revision](curved-contact-newton-readiness.json) preserves the geometry, loads, material and frozen acceptance limits. See the official v17 [contact principles](https://code-aster.org/doc/v17/manuals/man_u/u4/u4.44.11/Principes.html) and [pairing guidance](https://code-aster.org/doc/v17/manuals/man_u/u2/u2.04.04/Appariement.html).

Attempt 03 completed in 9.19 seconds without native alarms. An independent post-run audit then inspected the v17.4 contact-sample mapping and re-evaluated all 570 AUTO TRIA6 quadrature samples in each saved state. Code_Aster evaluates the continuous-contact gaps at those interior samples, then writes their values into node-indexed slots using source-ordered overwrite behavior. Comparing those values to a separate projection from the mesh nodes mixed two different locations; the original `contact-audit.json` therefore records a false open-gap discrepancy for that comparison.

The corrected [sample-matched gap review](curved-contact-attempt03/parent-contact-sample-gap-review.json) uses saved deformed coordinates and an independent quadratic-surface projector at the mapped sample locations. It passes all 12 states: maximum native-to-independent gap difference is about `1.3e-7 mm`; maximum penetration over all 570 samples is `0.002635 mm`, below the frozen `0.01 mm` limit. The source review is based on the official v17.4 tag; byte identity between that source and the pinned container executable has not been established. This audit resolves the node-versus-sample comparison issue, not curved pressure accuracy or full method acceptance.

The original audit's separate force comparison remains unresolved: summed nodal `RN` differs from the independently extracted cut `FORC_NODA` resultants by 10.0–26.8 N against state-specific limits of 0.118–0.398 N. The two cut-force resultants balance to `1.23e-12 N`. The [curved slave-cell order control](curved-contact-cell-order-control-attempt01/) now reverses only the 95 slave `TRIA6` record lines and runs the same deck in the same pinned image. At the loaded states, SCUT and MCUT resultants change by at most `5.13e-11 N`, and the saved displacement field changes by at most `1.09e-13 mm`; the reported summed `RN` changes by `0.984 N`, `7.00 N`, `1.02 N` and `0.984 N` at INST 5, 6, 7 and 11. Its RN-to-cut discrepancy remains `10.95–33.69 N` against the same `0.118–0.398 N` limits. This demonstrates source-order sensitivity of the curved output without demonstrating the cause of its absolute mismatch. Do not interpret the curved RN/cut difference as physical imbalance or mark it passed. The contact crop remains a bounded method screen; pressure accuracy, other interfaces, full-joint response and strength are unestablished.

The [parent LAGS_C reconstruction audit](curved-contact-attempt03/parent-lags-c-reconstruction-audit.json) adds a separate output-only diagnostic. A flat, fully active 3-D coupon reproduces the analytic `−3000 N` resultant to `4.6e-13 N`. Applying the same full-face six-point AUTO TRIA6 integration to curved attempt03 gives cut-resultant differences of `0.0387 N`, `0.3289 N`, `0.00112 N` and `0.0387 N` at INST 5, 6, 7 and 11, and the reversed order gives essentially the same values. This is empirical closure only: pinned-tag source review shows `STANDARD` assembles force on clipped-overlap FPG7 points using an augmented active-set traction and ray-gap derivative, not a full-face integral of raw `LAGS_C` times the exported master normal. The v17 manual also cautions against using `LAGS_C` directly for solution-quality postprocessing. These numbers neither reproduce the native residual nor pass curved pressure or force recovery; the original RN discrepancy remains open.

The [body-restricted force audit](curved-contact-body-force-audit.json) validates a different route for the *global* resultant. The flat fully-active PENTA15/TRIA6 known-answer calculates `FORC_NODA` on `S_SOLID` alone and recovers `+3000 N` at `SLNOD` within `4.7e-13 N`, with the expected opposite `RN` and same-body cut resultants. The paired curved TETRA10 run calculates the field separately on `BOLT`/`SNODE` and `WOOD`/`MNODE`, using identical decks, exports and runtime for the baseline and reversed slave-cell order. Across all 13 saved states, the two interface force resultants oppose within `1.24e-12 N`; each balances its own cut within `6.36e-14 N`, and each body's interface/cut first-moment residual stays below `1.46e-11 N·mm`. The body and cut fields change by at most about `7e-11 N` under cell-order reversal, while displacements change by `7.93e-14 mm`. The summed `RN` still differs from this body-restricted resultant by `10.0–26.8 N` in the baseline and `10.95–33.69 N` in the control, and changes by `0.984–6.998 N` under reversal. The body-restricted result is assembled from the same solved stress field, so it supplies a stable equilibrium/resultant route, not independent contact-pressure accuracy or residual replay. The approach follows the v17 [`CALC_CHAMP` force documentation](https://code-aster.org/doc/v17/manuals/man_u/u4/u4.81.04/index.html); the flat known-answer, both paired attempt folders, and preparation/audit scripts are linked from [`body_restricted_force`](../../../../fea/code_aster_trial/curved_contact/body_restricted_force/README.md).

## Three-dimensional contact output and traversal-order control

The flat 3-D coupon in [contact-3d-flat-attempt02](contact-3d-flat-attempt02/) passes all eleven frozen checks in the parent offline audit. At the final state, all nine slave contact nodes are active; the summed `CONT_NOEU/RN` vector is `[-2.38e-17, 1.62e-17, -3000] N`, matching the slave cut `REAC_NODA` vector to `4.55e-13 N`. The master and slave cut reaction z-resultants are equal and opposite at 3000 N. `LAGS_C` and `SIEF_NOEU.SIZZ` both match the analytic `-0.3 MPa` pressure. Attempt01's `SLAVE_CUT` table was accidentally extracted from `SLNOD` by a generic name-prefix branch, so it was not a cut-force result; attempt02 fixes the group mapping, and both frozen attempts remain preserved. This establishes the output route for a flat, fully active, conforming 3-D coupon only.

The mixed coupon shares three slave nodes between an active flat triangle and a raised, open triangle. In the original order (active face, then open face), all three shared nodes are reported open with zero `RN`; the summed `RN_z` is `-1016.901 N` while the slave cut reaction is `-1693.159 N`, a `676.258 N` difference. The open face's AUTO samples retain at least `0.4779 mm` initial clearance, so this is not a near-transition contact state. Reversing only the `SLAVE` group-member list did not change the output because the actual mesh-cell order stayed the same.

The effective control reversed the two slave TRIA6 cell records as well as the group listing, leaving labeled connectivity, coordinates, solid cells, solver command file, export file and pinned image unchanged. Code_Aster visited the same two slave mesh ordinals, 7 then 8; under this reversed input order the open face precedes the active face. The three shared-node statuses then report active and their `RN` slots become nonzero. The slave and master cut reaction components changed by at most `4.55e-13 N` between the paired runs, while the `RN_z`/slave-cut residual changed from `676.258 N` to zero. The independent [paired-output audit](contact-3d-mixed-cell-order-control-attempt01/parent-paired-order-audit.json) records all eight comparison checks passing.

This is direct evidence that source-ordered writes to shared-node contact output can hide earlier sample values and make the summed nodal field depend on traversal order in this small mixed fixture. It is not a force-capacity check, and agreement between `RN_z` and a cut `REAC_NODA` component in one ordering is not an acceptance criterion. In the reversed run, `RN_x/RN_y` still differ from the slave-cut components by about `[-17.724, +17.724] N`; this pair of imposed-displacement cuts has no full-vector analytic equilibrium oracle. The curved crop has now received its own record-order reversal: the cut forces and displacements remain invariant, while the nodal `RN` field changes and still fails its cut comparison. The mixed coupon therefore supports the output-overwrite mechanism, but neither it nor the curved control accounts for the curved crop's force mismatch. The next discriminating method step is a source-faithful residual replay, first verified on an existing flat fully active coupon and then on a small partial-overlap coupon with active and inactive residual quadrature points.

The observed order effect is consistent with the tagged v17.4 source: [reliem1](https://gitlab.com/codeaster/src/-/blob/17.4.0/bibfor/modelisa/reliem1.F90#L226) turns group membership into selected internal mesh ordinals and emits them in mesh order; [lrmast](https://gitlab.com/codeaster/src/-/blob/17.4.0/bibfor/modelisa/lrmast.F90#L547) and [stkmai](https://gitlab.com/codeaster/src/-/blob/17.4.0/bibfor/modelisa/stkmai.F90#L95) read the ASTER mesh subfiles sequentially and assign each declared cell its internal ordinal. [mmmres](https://gitlab.com/codeaster/src/-/blob/17.4.0/bibfor/cont_algo/mmmres.F90#L194) then visits the slave cells and writes contact values into node-indexed slots. The [group-list-only control](contact-3d-mixed-order-control-attempt01/) retained the same TRIA6 cell-record order and did not change those outputs. These tagged-source links are method evidence for v17.4; the binary in the pinned container was not byte-matched to this source checkout.

## Contact pressure extraction

Three frozen attempts in the pinned v17.4 image completed the nonlinear coupon history and explicit `SIEF_NOEU` calculation, then aborted inside `CALC_PRESSION` with a Fortran `CRTYPE` error, “Bad value during integer read.” The error also occurs when the macro is called for a single instant. Inspection of the official v17.4 macro source places the failure after stress extraction, during pressure-result construction/attachment; that location is the leading diagnosis, not a proven root cause. The attempts and their input freezes are preserved in `calc-pressure-known-answer-attempt01/`, `attempt02/` and `attempt03/`.

The documented underlying stress route did pass a separate [plane normal-stress known-answer](plane-contact-normal-stress-attempt02/). Direct `SIEF_NOEU.SIXX` values matched the analytic answer at all 14 states of an axis-aligned, 2-D plane-strain coupon: open states had zero stress, compressed states matched `−6` to `−24 MPa`, and integrating the two nodal values over the face recovered the six expected forces (60–240 N). Contact gap, contact force and independent end reaction also matched their analytic values; instrumentation changed none of the compared contact/reaction fields against the prior run. This validates stress extraction and its resultant only for this flat, X-normal coupon. It does not validate the failing `CALC_PRESSION` macro, curved 3-D pressure recovery, or joint acceptance.

## Evidence and next action

The [source freeze](source-freeze.json), per-attempt input freezes and [parent provenance audit](parent-provenance-audit.json) preserve both failed and successful attempts. The mass, map, flat-plane stress, flat 3-D output, body-restricted curved resultant and sample-matched gap audits can be reused within their stated limits. Next, validate a source-faithful contact-residual replay on an existing flat coupon, then add a minimal partial-overlap active/inactive coupon and validate local pressure recovery; only then proceed to a representative loaded joint patch. No candidate selection or physical work is authorized by these results.
