1. Authenticate the final current panel-receiver join, finished timber
   surface/axis register, proposed stock envelopes, attempt04 input manifest,
   complete contact graph and the existing all-body face-pair atlas. Verify
   their live byte hashes and transitive source closures. The atlas already

2. Reconcile the four moved `round_kicker_*_center_*` Hillman axes with their

   difference between nominal occupancy and installed thread engagement.
   The former inner-kicker backers are historical and absent.
3. Bind each kicker's rear inner edge to its saved panel source face, at the
   current seam X coordinate and rear plane Y = -36 mm. Reconcile all 97
   unordered kicker-to-other-body pairs from the 50-node graph: 19 broadphase
   candidates and 78 AABB-separated pairs. Preserve the 11 finite opposed
   pairs, four exact-separated candidates and four unresolved pairs. Separate
   direct rear timber backing from seam, lateral and top-panel contacts.
4. Project the existing overlap regions analytically. For direct rear
   contacts, accept only validated axis-aligned rectangular outlines with
   disjoint full circular holes: bind normals, plane, four line edges and
   endpoints, circular bounds/centers/lengths, and reconstructed area to the
   saved signature. Refuse unsupported trim or ambiguous geometry rather
   than substituting its bounding box. Report covered Z intervals, gaps and
   geometric stand-off to the closest finite rear support. Report a strip
   whose width is the saved panel thickness, and source-derived post-edge
   distances; these are reporting dimensions, not required bearing widths
   or an adopted support criterion. Keep curve/boundary closure and numerical
   source precision explicit.
5. Trace each current post's direct header seat separately from its
   post-to-cleat and cleat-to-header duties. The eight candidate bolts must
   retain their actual receiver pairs. Join the existing 84 center screw
   states from three rear cases without assigning those actions to individual
   downstream routes. Preserve missing six-case actions, active contact,
   stiffness, plywood/Hillman resistance and complete joint qualification.
6. Record focused synthetic checks, independent raw-record comparisons,
   final pins and three disjoint Luna/max reviews. Report dimensional remedy
   options and their required rechecks only if an actual support/path check
   establishes a revision need. No remedy changes the reviewed geometry.

## Boundaries

Continuous direct backing under the whole inner edge is not an explicit
adopted criterion. A direct-backing gap is geometry evidence, not a failed
panel/support check or a mandate to add a continuous member. Finite CAD
contact does not establish an active reaction, force sharing or capacity.
The center seam and zero-area cleat tangencies remain distinct from direct
timber backing.

The source audit currently supports a fully metadata-only projection; no CAD
launch is planned. If the saved signatures cannot support a required result,
retain that refusal and send a concrete frozen input/query plan to the primary
before any CAD. Heavy-run readiness, serialization, native execution, final
integration and Git remain primary-owned. No blanket new inspection, floor
test or external sign-off prerequisite is introduced.
