# Current proposed stock reconciliation

This packet joins proposed starting stock for all 20 frame timbers and 24 connector blocks to the reviewed current finished geometry. All 44 original envelopes and the four reduced sections contain their finished solids. It adds 15 bounded stock-length scenarios while preserving the original section of each remnant.

- [Envelope geometry and provenance](envelopes.md) explain the proposed grain/section frames, exact STEP containment, source pins and limits.
- [Current piece schedule and cut scenarios](cut-scenarios.md) cover all 44 blanks, the four later section rips and the five unplaced pieces in the 8 ft-only scenarios.
- [Length-nesting method](nesting.md) records the deterministic heuristic, per-blank kerf and end-trim accounting.
- [Independent review](review.md) records validation and findings.

These are conditional geometry and arithmetic results for `compact-floor-flush-wood-joints-development`, revision `led-clearance-2x6-runner-seated-blocks-v1`. They do not recover saved raw-host geometry, select purchased lumber, assign post-rip grade, complete machining or resistance checks, record actual inspections, or release physical work. The existing partial timber packets remain historical inputs, with their original scopes and manifest identities preserved.

Public files are the Python producers/helpers, focused tests and these notes. Raw JSON and exact source STEP files remain local and ignored. The replay commands and full identities are in the linked notes.
