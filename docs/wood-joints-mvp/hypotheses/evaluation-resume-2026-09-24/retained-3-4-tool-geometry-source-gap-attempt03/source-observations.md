# Source observations — attempt03

Search date: 2026-09-28. Exact target: Wera 6000 Joker Imperial, part
`05073287001`, nominal size 3/4 in.

The official [Wera family product page](https://www.wera.de/en/tools/6000-joker-ratcheting-combination-wrenches-imperial)
lists the target variant and these scalar dimensions: 246 mm overall length,
42 mm open-end external width, 9.5 mm open-end head thickness, 34.8 mm
ratchet-end external width and 11 mm maximum ratchet-head height. It also lists
the 30-degree return feature and 80-tooth ring ratchet. No datum-based complete
outline, contour coordinates, tolerance scheme or exact-part geometry download
is exposed in the reviewed page content.

The product page's [linked datasheet](https://hybris-media.wera.de/download/pdfgenerator-datasheets/en/05073280001.pdf)
opened as a 3-page PDF with cover identity `05073280001` (5/16 in). Its family
table includes the exact `05073287001` row and corroborates the scalar
dimensions. It is not a target-cover datasheet or a profile drawing. Its cover
packaging dimensions belong to `05073280001` and are not applied to the 3/4-in
tool. The [linked user manual](https://www.wera.de/fileadmin/pdf/manuals/11670984-00000177-09_screen.pdf)
opened as a 21-page family manual and describes use/features, including the
holding plate and stop; it supplies no complete dimensioned profile.

The official [catalog index](https://www.wera.de/en/downloads/catalogs)
opened and listed the 2026 Compact Catalogue and 2024 Joker salesfolder. The
linked 2026 catalogue is
[EN-Compact-Catalogue-02-2026.pdf](https://www.wera.de/fileadmin/pdf/catalog/EN-Compact-Catalogue-02-2026.pdf)
(41,476,567 bytes); the browser reader rejected it as too large. The linked
Joker folder is
[EN-Joker-Salesfolder.pdf](https://www.wera.de/fileadmin/pdf/joker/EN-Joker-Salesfolder.pdf)
(26,852,721 bytes); it was also rejected as too large. Wera-hosted indexed
text from the 2025 compact catalogue and Joker folder exposes product-table
dimensions and images, not a fully dimensioned contour. No claim is made about
unparsed pages of the oversized files.

The exact-part PDF routes
[`https://www.wera.de/05073287001.pdf`](https://www.wera.de/05073287001.pdf)
and
[`https://hybris-media.wera.de/download/pdfgenerator-datasheets/en/05073287001.pdf`](https://hybris-media.wera.de/download/pdfgenerator-datasheets/en/05073287001.pdf)

outcome, not proof that the corresponding files do or do not exist. The exact
article page's short `.pdf` route is similarly unresolved.

Wera's [Digital Support PDF](https://www.wera.de/sh/Wera-Digital-World.pdf)
says the Wera Product Finder may include AR/VR product imagery and that Wera
has a virtual product image experience. The official [Wera AR page](https://www.wera.de/en/experience-wera/wera-ar/)
describes generic Wera tools as rotatable 3D/AR objects. Neither source
identifies an AR/CAD object for `05073287001`, provides a model download, or
binds scale, datum, model revision and tolerances. The exact family product
page's rendered content exposed no AR/3D link or statement. Generic digital
viewing therefore remains a discovery lead, not accepted measurable exact-part
geometry.

The official [Data Cockpit page](https://www.wera.de/en/data-cockpit) and
[Data Cockpit host](https://data.wera.de/) did not expose an accessible part
asset in this browser session. Wera's [media database](https://media.wera.de/)
displayed a login page. No credentials were available or used, and there was
no vendor contact. The Wera documentation describes these channels as product
data/media access but does not establish a public exact-part CAD download.

Exact-part searches were also run against TraceParts, 3Dfindit and
PARTcommunity domains. No `05073287001` model result was surfaced and no
manufacturer statement authorizing any of those portals for this exact item was
found. General CAD portal results were excluded. The third-party Olander “View
3D CAD Model” label preserved in attempt02 remains unqualified and was not used
as geometry evidence.

The web tool returned page-rendered text, parsed PDF text and access errors; it
did not provide original HTTP/PDF bytes as local files. This record therefore
hashes only the local packet artifacts. It does not assign a hash to a Wera
source whose bytes were not retrieved.
