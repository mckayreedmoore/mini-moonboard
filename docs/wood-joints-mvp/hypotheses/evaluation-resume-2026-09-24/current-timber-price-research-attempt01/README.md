# Denver-area timber price lookup, attempt 01

**Checked:** 2026-09-27. **Result:** no usable local price observed. This is a
public-page lookup, not a quote or product selection, for candidate
`led-clearance-2x6-runner-seated-blocks-v1` and the [current source/yield
screen][schedule].

## Findings

- **2×6:** Front Range Lumber's Metro Denver framing page lists 2×6 lengths
  from 8 to 20 ft, but publishes no item price and says availability must be
  checked by location. Lowe's lists a 2×6×8 #2 Prime Douglas-fir, kiln-dried
  item (model 720966-8; actual 1.5×5.5 in); it also says anti-stain treated.
  Its page shows “Get Pricing & Availability” and asks for a ZIP/city; no price
  appeared. Home Depot lists a 2×6×8 #2 Premium Grade Fir, kiln-dried,
  untreated item (model D206SEC.189.08), but the page had no store selected and
  showed no price. Its listing also says species may vary by region.
- **4×4:** Front Range lists Douglas-fir 4×4 in 8, 10, 12, 16 and 20 ft, with
  no item price. Lowe's lists a 4×4×8 #2 Premium Douglas-fir green item
  (item/model 4023; actual 3.563×3.563 in); pricing is behind its ZIP/city
  prompt. Home Depot lists a 4×4×8 #2 Premium Grade Douglas-fir item
  (model 279542; actual 3.5×3.5 in); its page says “Select store” and gives no
  price in the checked view.
- **4×6:** Front Range lists Douglas-fir 4×6 in 8, 10, 12 and 16 ft, with no
  item price. Lowe's lists a 4×6×8 #2 & Better Douglas-fir green item
  (model 637637; actual 3.562×5.625 in; S4S; anti-stain treated); its page
  shows the same ZIP/city pricing gate and no amount. Home Depot's
  #2 Premium Grade Fir 4×6×8 page also had no selected store or visible price.

Front Range identifies its pages as Metro Denver + Northern Colorado, but
says listed stock is not guaranteed and that items may not be at both yards.
The Lowe's and Home Depot product pages are retailer listings, not evidence
that these SKUs are stocked at a Denver-area store. I did not select a store or
enter a ZIP code, so no personalized local inventory or price was returned.
The product-specific facts above describe listing text, not inspected stock.

## Price fields still unresolved

There is no observed current unit price for any of the three stock classes,
nor a local quote for the source schedule's required lengths and quantities.
Still unknown are the per-piece price by SKU and length; Denver pickup versus
delivery charges; applicable tax; bulk pricing or minimums; and the delivered
cost after choosing a product. The schedule's purchase count and usable yield
also remain open: its lengths are not a nesting/cut plan, and four proposed
4×6 block blanks require cross-section rips. No price or yield was inferred
from nominal dimensions or linear footage.

These listings do not resolve candidate fit. Confirm the exact delivered
species group, grade, treatment, moisture and receiving condition separately.
In particular, a grade stamp on a 4×6 blank does not by itself settle the
disposition of the four proposed ripped blocks. No product is selected or
accepted by this note.

## Reproducible method and source record

On 2026-09-27, searched and opened the primary retailer product/listing pages
below for 2×6, 4×4 and 4×6 stock, then read the visible page fields for size,
length, grade, species, treatment/dryness, location prompt and price. No
account, ZIP, store selection, cart, supplier contact or transaction was used.
The date is the observation date; the pages did not provide a reliable
price-effective timestamp. Recheck the same pages with the intended Denver ZIP
and store before treating a future displayed amount as a local price.

- Local yard: [Front Range timber sizes][frl-timbers], [framing sizes][frl-framing],
  and [Lakewood location][frl-lakewood]. No item-level prices were displayed.
- Lowe's: [2×6×8 #2 Prime kiln-dried Douglas-fir][lowes-2x6],
  [4×4×8 #2 Premium green Douglas-fir][lowes-4x4], and
  [4×6×8 #2 Better green Douglas-fir][lowes-4x6]. Each page required a ZIP/city
  for pricing and availability; no dollar amount was displayed in the checked
  view.
- Home Depot: [2×6×8 #2 Premium Grade Fir][hd-2x6],
  [4×4×8 #2 Premium Grade Fir][hd-4x4], and
  [4×6×8 #2 Premium Grade Fir][hd-4x6]. The checked pages showed “Select
  store” and no dollar amount. Home Depot states local prices may vary and
  inventory is not guaranteed.
