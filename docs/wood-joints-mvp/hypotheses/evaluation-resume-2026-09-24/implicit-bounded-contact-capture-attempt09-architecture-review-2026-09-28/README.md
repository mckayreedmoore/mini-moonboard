## Findings

The patch changes only three pinned source members: `nonlingeo.c`, `gencontelem_f2f.f`, and `ccx_2.23.c`. The diff has 794 added lines and no deletions. I replayed it against the packet’s 1,197-file archived source with `patch --batch --fuzz=0 --verbose -p1`; it applied without offsets or fuzz, and all three modified-member digests matched `source-pins.json` and `patch-preparation.json`. The replay changed only a temporary extraction under `/tmp`.

The hook placement is coherent with the captured lifecycle. `contact.c` reaches the pinned Fortran generator. The generation-begin hook precedes the second, in-loop `contact()` call and is guarded to the implicit mortar branch; it does not instrument the earlier seed call. The generator samples capture activity once, and only active capture executes the face/point bookkeeping. With no path configured, the cached disabled state skips that bookkeeping and the post-results spring scan; this is a structural review of the disabled path, not an overhead benchmark. After the results copy, the guarded post-results block captures corrected displacement and reads spring trial values. The iteration link follows `checkconvergence()`. The single job finalizer follows the complete step loop and `closefile`. Line-numbered pinned-source excerpts are in [`source-callsite-evidence.txt`](source-callsite-evidence.txt).

The sink owns process-local static state and has no lock or rank aggregation. It lazily validates seven SHA-256 bindings and bounded tie/face rosters, creates a PID-suffixed temporary sibling with exclusive-create mode, and obtains cleanup ownership only after that open succeeds. It flushes and closes before publishing through a no-overwrite hard link. A shared destination across processes can publish only once; the losing writer fails closed. Each process/job therefore needs a distinct output path. The pinned build recipe does not enable `CALCULIX_MPI`, but any future threaded or MPI build remains outside the verified scope.

The writer and reader agree on the declared stream bounds: 128 MiB total output, 2,048-byte records, 64 generations, 250,000 combined pass-1 and observed pass-2 candidate rows per generation, 13,001 expected faces, 128 ties, 999,999 integration points, and 1,000,000 state doubles. The reader rejects malformed encodings, unknown records, inconsistent identities and counts, missing links/footer, cap overruns, and incomplete capture flags. State joins use exact displacement comparison; FNV digests are auxiliary. Empty-current and zero-to-zero domains are addressed by the frozen attempt09 coverage additions.

One disclosed limitation remains: the writer checks live post-hook `iit`, but `ITERATION_LINK` does not serialize that value, so the reader cannot independently repeat the `iit` check. The reader does validate the serialized convergence/attempt transition and required capture flags. Its `PASS_CAPTURE_STRUCTURE` status is explicitly structural; an accepted link means source convergence/cutback convention only, not joint acceptance, native mechanics, force-history demand, or load-path transfer. Runtime contact census does not prove geometric search completeness or first local bearing. The contract marks the current joint unselected and requires parent rebinding of the exact input/include closure, source/patch/binary, ordered tie and per-tie face rosters, and law/energy settings.

The 128 MiB ceiling bounds wire bytes, not peak parser memory: `capture_reader.py` decodes and materializes text, lines, and split fields. This is a non-blocking resource caveat for the declared offline lane; the packet contains no separate parser-memory budget or streaming reader.
