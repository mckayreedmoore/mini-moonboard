# Independent review — attempt04 AWC bibliography boundary

**Verdict: SUPPORTED_WITH_NARROW_WORDING_NOTE.** The edition distinctions and
the conclusion that no official 2024 References 39–41 mapping was recovered are
supported. No capacity, method, criterion, or readiness overclaim was found.

## Findings

1. The official AWC FAQ is dated September 27, 2021. Its displayed entries
   39–41 name AISC 1985, AISC 1989, and AISI 1996. The direct page request
   returned 403 during this review; the entry text was available in AWC's
   indexed result. This is evidence of what that FAQ displays, not an
   edition-authenticated 2024 bibliography.
2. The official AWC [2018 NDS References PDF](https://awc.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/AWC_NDS2018-withCommentary_20200827_AWCWebsite_References.pdf)
   was readable. Printed page 191 lists ref. 39 as RCSC 2009, ref. 40 as
   ANSI/AISC 360-10 (2010), and ref. 41 as AISI's 2007 North American Standard
   for Cold-Formed Steel Framing. The attempt04 transcription is accurate for
   this edition.
3. AWC's [2024 NDS resource page](https://awc.org/resources/2024-nds/)
   identifies the 2024 standard and advertises a free view-only option, but
   the retrieved page does not expose the References pages. It cannot verify
   the 2024 numbering. Leaving the mirror-derived assignments provisional is
   correct.
4. Attempt04 is a bibliography-boundary screen only. It emits no capacity,
   evaluates no design method, changes no criterion or release state, and
   does not claim that a method cannot exist. Its `SOURCE_GAP` disposition is
   consistent with attempt03 and is not represented as a new mechanics result.

**Wording note:** “The discrepancy demonstrates that numbering and editions
vary across NDS editions” is directionally reasonable, but the FAQ does not
identify the edition whose references it reproduces. Prefer: “The FAQ's
displayed entries differ from the official 2018 list, so the FAQ is not an
edition-specific basis for the 2024 mapping.” This does not change attempt04's
decision.
