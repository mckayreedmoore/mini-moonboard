# Source addendum: imposed-actuator work in native energy checks

CCX 2.21's implicit-dynamic `allwk` does not include work done by the reaction at a prescribed-displacement actuator target. In `nonlingeo.c` (lines 3174–3178), the solver resets `allwk` to its start-of-increment value and calls `worparll`. That routine accumulates the trapezoidal work of `fnext` and `fnextini` against the change in translational displacement (`worparll.c`, lines 93–96). The `fnext` vector is assembled from applied point and distributed loads (`mafillsmforc.f`, lines 55–63; `mafillsm.f`, lines 520–530); it is not the support-reaction vector for prescribed boundary motion. A prescribed *BOUNDARY value on the massless endpoint driving the internal `SPRING2` therefore does not add the reaction-times-target-displacement work to `allwk`.

That omission matters to energy-based decisions. `checkimpacts.f` forms its absolute and relative energy residuals from internal, kinetic, contact and friction energy minus `allwk`, damping work and the reference energy (lines 90–107). For surface-to-surface contact, its contact branch still tests the rebound/persistent-contact variation `r_rel - r_rel_bc > 0.0025` when `ne <= neini` (lines 146–166). `checkconvergence.c` explicitly comments that energy/work can be incorrect for forced boundary conditions and disables one no-contact divergence response (lines 217–240), but the separate contact strategy has no `idrct == 0` guard (line 256) and calls `checkimpacts` (lines 274–278). The source therefore supports that omitted positive controller work can make the energy residual too positive and can influence contact time-step/retry decisions.

This is a plausible contributor to the current retries, not a proved cause for a particular increment. The parent-observed native `allwk = 0` and positive independently reconstructed actuator target work are consistent with the source path, but the captured prefix does not preserve the exact `r_rel`, `r_rel_bc`, `delta_r_rel`, and `emax` values needed to show which `checkimpacts` branch fired. The log also reports contact-element stabilization separately. Do not attribute every cutback to work omission without reconstructing those inputs.

For interpreting this diagnostic, retain the solver-reported `allwk` and residual as the native load-vector accounting, and report a separate controller-inclusive balance using the independently recovered actuator reaction work. Do not call native `allwk` total external work for this prescribed-target model, and do not relax energy tolerances merely to make the run progress. A future solver-energy check that includes this drive needs validated reaction-work accounting or a different verified driving formulation; applying `DIRECT` alone is not established as a cure, since it was checked only in a no-contact fixture while this contact branch remains active.

This complements [the stiffness-adaptation review](current-native-stiffness-adaptation-review.md); neither note makes a mechanics-acceptance claim.
