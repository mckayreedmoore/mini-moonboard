The current official [Roseburg Exterior Core page](https://www.roseburg.com/softwood-plywood/exterior-core/) describes the family as western-wood-veneer panels with core/back grade C or better and presents the current **AC Sanded Plywood** option card as 23/32 in, five ply, 24-in on-center, with a fully sanded face and touch-sanded back (rendered lines 25–28 and 132–135). The page also lists AC face-grade details, generic core/back information, and a broad APA/PS 1 statement (lines 45–95 and 107–115). It names neither Lowe's item 12235 nor model 119055.

Roseburg's official [Sanded Plywood brochure](https://www.roseburg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SandedPlywood_Brochure.pdf) has an AC Sanded section and lists AC-family face A, core C (two-piece or composed), core gaps per APA PS-1, and back C. Its Exterior Core packaging table gives the 23/32-in row as **5 & 7 ply** (PDF page P4, rendered lines 182–230). That is a family packaging range; it does not name Lowe's identifiers or provide an exact model veneer schedule. The section string “AC SANDED 013026” is not defined there as a manufacturer product code. Roseburg's general softwood-plywood page also says APA trademarked/compliant with PS 1, but this is separate from Lowe's PS1-09 listing field and does not establish model-specific edition compliance.

The attempt06 direct-source observations and exact-ID searches therefore support the bounded finding “no direct mapping surfaced in this reviewed set.” This is not proof that a cross-reference does not exist in other public, manufacturer, retailer, order, or lot records. Descriptor similarity, the Lowe's URL path, and the generic Lowe's-hosted Roseburg SDS cannot establish exact model identity. The reviewed pages and brochure provide no model-specific numeric structural design values or orthotropic properties.
