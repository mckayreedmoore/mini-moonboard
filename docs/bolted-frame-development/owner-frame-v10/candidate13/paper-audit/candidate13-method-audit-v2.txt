Primary-method findings

NDS2024 sections11.3.1 and11.3.6 apply applicable adjustment factors, including Cg, to lateral connection design value Z. The row definition requires load alignment; an identical receiver tuple is not that definition. Section11.2.3 requires independent metal design and excludes NDS adjustments from metal strength. The current implementation's receiver-tuple1/n is therefore an explicitly unqualified diagnostic, not an established row factor. Actual unequal FE forces do not automatically waive Cg. Equally, applying1/n to every already-resolved individual pressure cannot be assumed equivalent to the specified group-capacity calculation.

The bearing screen is not unadjusted Fe stress. Its4Ktheta matches Mode-I Rd in Table12.3.1B. But pointwise affine pressure divided by this term is not the complete minimum of applicable yield modes required by12.3.1. The shaft N/M screen uses plastic section d^3/6 with a uniform /4 and diagnostic Cg; a complete code derivation for that combined criterion has not been established. Keep it distinct from independent elastic steel reference .422766. No criterion is deleted or favorably re-factored here.

TR12 provides single-fastener generalized lateral yield methods, including gaps and varying shaft diameters; Z is the minimum mode result divided by the appropriate reduction term. It does not establish that the present prescribed affine pressures and free couples are an equivalent whole-joint yield solution. Its edition is copyright2015, based on2012NDS; current2024NDS controls applicable provisions.

Plywood bearing is a separate material audit from panel net/perimeter strength. TR12 Appendix A supports5600psi for plywood bolts above1/4inch across grades and grain directions. The inherited3150psi transverse assumption is lower; it is a conservative hypothesis, not that table value. The2024NDS small-fastener plywood table applies only through1/4inch. The half-inch APA strength/stiffness audit does not itself validate dowel bearing or perfect bonding. No factor is changed.

Minimal remaining analysis and explicit blocker

Build the actual receiver/shear-plane/wrench atlas, then calculate every applicable NDS/TR12 yield mode with actual bearing lengths, gap/body/root intervals, load angles and justified main/side designations. Establish group load direction, stiffness areas and full group membership; apply the smallest applicable geometry factor consistently. Check eccentric tension-perpendicular-to-grain, splitting/tear-out/net sections, axial head/nut/washer load transfer and independent metal limit states. Validate physical screw force-slip/resistance, plywood bonds and floor supports. No new global solve is needed merely to audit these equations; changed joint constitutive assumptions would require fresh response evidence.

No new complete Z is reported here: assigning simple shear planes while omitting measured free couples or unequal media would invent the missing applicability. This is a precise method/input blocker, not lack of solver convergence.
