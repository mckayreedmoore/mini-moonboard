Candidate 13: saved mechanics and group-factor scope audit
Paper audit only. No repository promotion, global rerun, load change, resistance
change, fabrication release, climbing release or structural acceptance.

Finding

The force model already generates unequal simultaneous fastener actions. It
does not prescribe equal sharing and does not contain an explicit Cg or 1/n
capacity reduction in its equilibrium equations. Applying a second reduction
solely to reproduce the SAME load-distribution effect would duplicate that
effect under a narrowly defined, qualified scalar group model. The present
three-dimensional model is not that qualified scalar model. Its foundation
stiffness, gross member/plate approximations, interpolation and physical inputs
are not validated bounds. Therefore this audit does not justify Cg=1, remove
any of the 26 saved bearing-trial flags, or establish a whole-joint pass.

1. What the actual candidate 13 force model resolves

Timber: independent, gross rectangular, linear 3D Timoshenko beams. The declared
DF-L scenario uses E=11031.612 MPa and G/E=0.064, with beam segments at most
150 mm. Axial EA/L, bending EI, transverse shear GA and torsion GJ couple the
fastener ports through each member. Point translations and rotations are
interpolated between beam nodes and retain eccentric rigid arms. Thus common
member extension affects fastener sharing, but a short bolt row within one
150 mm segment is not an independently refined sequence of exact NDS row
segments. Gross beam stiffness does not include local hole stress fields or
net-section compliance. Net-section resistance is a separate postprocessor.
Source: scripts/thin_bolted_frame_mechanics.py lines93-223.

Bonded cleats: two nominal 12.7 mm plywood leaves become one 25.4 mm analytical
body under perfect compatibility. Separate published half-inch EA and apparent
flatwise EI, parallel-axis composite action and the retained in-plane shear
surrogate are used. The trapezoidal body is approximated by a full-width beam.
Adhesive qualification, delamination, actual stiffness bounds and force-slip
behavior are not established. Source: scripts/eoere_v10_thin_cleat.py and
thin-stiffness-input.json, both frozen in the candidate inputs.

Shafts: independent nominal-diameter circular linear elastic Timoshenko shafts,
E=200000 MPa, nu=0.3, shear factor 0.9. Nodes include actual surface boundaries,
midspans, end seats and nominal segments at most 25 mm. Their bending response
couples the actual separate wood and steel bearing intervals. The global force
model does not represent physical thread-root/transition stiffness, yielding,
plastic hinges, damage or a delivered-shank bound. Those diameter/material
resistance scenarios are separate checks. Source:
scripts/thin_bolted_common_shaft.py lines111-241.

Wood/steel bearing springs: two axial Gauss points per actual bearing interval.
Each has independent radial slip relative to its own receiver/angle port and
its own bore radial clearance c=(boreD-majorD)/2. With lateral slip vector d,
r=norm(d), spring force is zero for r<=c and k(1-c/r)d for r>c. This is a
radial dead-zone law with linear magnitude beyond engagement; its direction,
active-set changes and consistent tangent make the global problem nonlinear.
It is NOT nonlinear wood crushing or a plastic embedment law. The force model
uses a common isotropic wood foundation 26.2467191601 N/mm^2 times Gauss length
weight, and steel foundation 1799.77502812 N/mm^2 times length weight. Grain
angle affects subsequent resistance calculations, not this isotropic wood
foundation coefficient. Axial stiffness of these radial springs is zero.
Sources: thin_bolted_common_shaft.py lines214-241;
thin_bolted_frame_mechanics.py lines305-323; first_order_factory.py lines237-241.

Axial capture: separate compression-only center captures at actual end/host
support stations, stiffness 1000 N/mm. They transfer axial reactions but do not
resolve washer pressure, rotational clamping, preload, friction or prying.
They are included in stored surface wrenches; the affine lateral bearing
tractions use radial springs only. Sources: thin_bolted_common_shaft.py
lines232-241 and328-352.

Angles: actual near and far bolt ports are independently controlled by the
48 DOF eight-port linear gross half-strip operator and condensed rigid heel.
This includes finite angle-strip deformation and work-conjugate eccentric
load paths. It is not physical plate, bend-root, loaded-hole or local steel
yield qualification. Interior contact uses the declared unloaded heel-lift
Ritz kinematics; contact-dependent heel condensation and finite-motion
accuracy are not established. Source: scripts/eoere_v10_fitting.py and its
frozen provenance/controls, rather than inherited four-port descriptive text.

Direct contacts: area-weighted compression-only bedding, with separation
allowed, retain actual owner pairs. Candidate 13 added no owner pair relative
to 11b; each existing side/cleat patch grew from 34403.3732 to 35943.9879 mm^2
while its cells grew 18 to 75. Every changed cell has k=area*bedding exactly;
the summed stiffness grew 4.48%, not fourfold. These are actual-source contact
priors, not proven physical pressure/slip laws. See contact-delta-13.json and
contact-area-weight-check.json. Floor no-slip and panel/screw assumptions
remain the same unqualified physical conditions as the completed bank.

Unequal actions therefore arise jointly from member extension/bending, shaft
bending and interval length, angle compliance, eccentric moment, independent
bore engagement, contact opening/closure, asymmetric loading and the global
load path. No scalar Cg is required for the solver to produce unequal actions.
It nevertheless lacks a calibrated single-fastener load-deformation law,
orthotropic local continuum response, wood crushing/yield, brittle splitting,
row/group tear-out, local notch effects, fastening tolerances and actual
fabrication/material observations. Equilibrium cannot establish these.

It audits all 13 flagged axes in all 6 simultaneous cases:

Artifacts: audit_saved_mechanics.py and saved-point-resultant-audit.json.
The independent group-force-atlas-13.json provides the simultaneous 52 exact

Shared-host/steel adjacency is not asserted to be a single NDS design group.

3. Distribution overlap: exact algebra and its limits

For ONLY a parallel positive scalar group with equal individual resistance Z,
fixed load fractions alpha_i>=0, sum(alpha_i)=1 and group force P:

  max individual index = alpha_max*P/Z
  Cg_distribution = 1/(n*alpha_max)
  group index P/(n*Cg_distribution*Z) = alpha_max*P/Z.

Dividing the already unequal individual index by that SAME distribution
factor again repeats the distribution allowance. This identity is not an
adopted factor for candidate 13. Vector forces, different bearing directions,
multi-interval shafts, group moments, end reactions, contact paths, different
resistances and uncalibrated compliance prevent its direct adoption here.
Signed/vector cancellation also makes a simple load fraction unsuitable as
a general capacity or Cg bound.

The saved universal 1/n trial is keyed by identical complete surface signature,
not an independently qualified NDS row. Its conservative purpose is to retain
an unresolved group-distribution allowance. It is not evidence that the code
requires an additional arbitrary n-fold local bearing-strength reduction.
Conversely the present mechanics is not evidence that all prescribed group
adjustments can be discarded. A source-supported application decision must
identify the actual connection/row, the design value being adjusted, member
EA and spacing, applicable single-fastener slip/modulus assumptions, load
direction, and all other governing geometry/strength mechanisms.

The old flag list remains unchanged. A fixed-force parameter comparison
between unadjusted individual references and the 1/n trial is interpretation
of two declared postprocessing hypotheses, not a physical sensitivity bound.
Changing spring stiffness, crushing, preload, support or contact assumptions
could redistribute forces and cannot be evaluated by reusing the same forces
as if they were new global solutions. No such rerun was done.

4. Primary engineering scope

American Wood Council, Design of Bolted Connections per the 2015 NDS:
https://awc.org/resources/design-of-bolted-connections-per-the-2015-nds/
AWC explains Cg as accounting for unequal group load distribution and treats
spacing/geometry adjustments separately. This explanatory source does not
itself qualify the present 2024 NDS oblique mixed-material arrangements.

USDA Forest Products Laboratory, Rammer 2006, Wood Construction Codes Issues
in the United States, section 2.1.2 and 2.2:
https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/27033.pdf
The research distinguishes elastic distribution assumptions involving member
extension and single-fastener deformation from wood limit states and local
crushing redistribution; it separately describes brittle wood failure modes.

American Wood Council Technical Report 12, sections 1.1-1.3:
https://web-media.awc.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/17210714/AWC-TR12-1510.pdf
The generalized dowel yield equations address single-fastener yield-limit
references; spacing, group action, member strength and end-use adjustments
remain separate design considerations. This supports separating force
distribution, local resistance and complete-joint qualification.

No source in this audit establishes that a Cg adjustment to a group lateral
design value can automatically be applied as an identical multiplier on local
elastic bearing stress or material Fe. The companion joint applicability
review owns the exact current NDS clause assessment. Until that assessment and
physical applicability are supported, retain every trial flag and hold.
