Conclusion
Retain all 26 above-one trial rows (13 distinct components) as unresolved numerical diagnostic flags. Their maximum is 1.9601589576, left rear-leg bolt2, a12-rear, including the imposed Cg=0.5. The unpenalized local reference is 0.9800794788. Neither number establishes physical connection failure or a whole-connection pass. The defensible status is an unqualified, deliberately severe local-bearing screening diagnostic; calling it a universally conservative whole-joint bound goes beyond the demonstrated scope.

What the code actually does
The matched six-case worksheet contains696 rows. eoere_v10_reference.py lines139–147 groups shafts solely by identical sorted (surface kind,host) tuples, assigning1/n. This is not a load-row classification. Lines150–176 reconstruct vector affine line pressure from each host force and transverse moment, preserving their resultants. Lines208–239 retain affine sawn/steel pressures and replace plywood pressure by balanced blocks; they check force/moment closure. Lines225–234 split at the assumed body/root transition and evaluate the local whole-interval pressure comparison, then divide by1/n. scripts/eoere_bearing_envelope.py lines22–32 already uses Hankinson-interpolated Fe and4Ktheta, with Ktheta=1+theta/(2pi). Thus the before-Cg number is an ASD-reduced, Mode-I-like local reference, not raw material Fe utilization. Its mathematical interval enclosure is a bound on that chosen formula, not proof of physical bore pressure or connection capacity.

The FE law is materially different from a strength model. scripts/thin_bolted_common_shaft.py lines191–205 assembles elastic beam shafts; lines217–231 use two Gauss-point foundation interactions per receiver span, scalar foundation stiffness and radial clearance. Declared physical stiffness/pressure qualifications are false. This solves unequal compatibility forces and transverse couples in the assumed system; it does not demonstrate crushing, plastic hinges, splitting, or ultimate redistribution. The reference's affine reconstruction preserves these integrated actions but is not independently calibrated contact pressure.

Primary provisions and scope
2024NDS11.3.6.1 applies Cg to lateral reference design value Z. Its equation depends on number in a row, EA, spacing and load-slip modulus;11.3.6.2 defines same-diameter, load-aligned dowel rows and adjacent-stagger merging;11.3.6.3 specifies gross/equivalent areas. It does not prescribe receiver-signature1/n or a factor for an arbitrary local affine stress. No general perpendicular-to-grain exemption justifies setting Cg=1. NDS11.2.2 also conditions summation on same-type/similar-size fasteners with the same yield mode and separately requires local-stress checks.

TR12 sections1.2–1.3 concern single-fastener lateral yield values and all applicable yield modes. Distinguish wood embedment Fe, steel tensile Fy, dowel bending Fyb and connection allowable Z. Applying Fe/(4Ktheta) locally is an engineering extension of a Mode-I-like limit; it is not automatically min(all applicable P/Rd) for a mixed, multiport, moment-loaded stack. The shaft N/M screen uses Fy/4, plastic section modulus D^3/6, and the imposedCg; direct elastic steel uses Fy/2 independently. NDS11.2.3 does support keeping independent metal design separate from wood adjustment factors, but passing this particular elastic steel screen does not qualify all hardware failure modes or validate the reduced N/M extension.

Does the group factor double count?
There is an overlap risk: the group factor's elastic load-distribution rationale and the compatibility FE both concern unequal fastener participation. Yet elastic FE sharing is not a substitute for the standard's adjusted connection-strength procedure. A blanket declaration that Cg is double counted and may be deleted is unsupported. Conversely, multiplying already unequal local pressures by the receiver count is not demonstrated to be the NDS-prescribed group check. Within this unchanged local formula the imposed penalty is numerically conservative relative to no penalty. That narrow algebraic statement does not prove a universal lower capacity bound for all oblique, eccentric or shared-host joints. In particular opposite-signed force components and couples defeat a simple resultant/count argument.

Review of other proposed dispositions
I agree with candidate13-group-factor-method-audit-v1.json on retaining the flags without calling them confirmed physical failures or passes. Its phrase 'unqualified conservative local bearing diagnostic' is acceptable only with the narrow formula-relative meaning above. The independent saved-point wrench/shaft closure supports extraction correctness; it is not capacity validation. Changing plywood Fe to a larger primary tabulated value might be justified for an applicable specified panel, but it cannot be selected to erase these flags or qualify the actual thin laminated cleat, its adhesive, splitting and force-slip response. The inherited3150 perpendicular value is a conservative material hypothesis, not evidence that the actual assembly is qualified.

Center-principal068/069 near-edge condition remains open. The approximately33.704mm center distance is below the38.1mm full4D marker for3/8inch bolts. A net force away from an edge cannot erase reverse/local outward pressure or a free couple; the reported affine reversals in major cases make that shortcut especially weak. Conversely a signed affine endpoint is not a measured local contact field and alone does not establish the exact applicable NDS loaded-edge rule. Resolve the actual load/plane/member and eccentric/oblique applicability, including geometry factor and splitting, rather than assign a favorable edge category. NDS11.1.2 requires local member stress evaluation and11.1.3 specifically addresses eccentric perpendicular-to-grain tension.

Minimal next work, without a global force rerun
1. Freeze a joint-by-joint map of all member/steel planes, signed forces AND couples, rows, diameters, spacings, gross/equivalent EA and required stagger merging for every six-case demand. Include all shared-host fasteners, not only identical receiver tuples.
2. Establish applicable NDS/TR12 single-fastener Z from all valid yield modes and actual bearing lengths, gaps, body/root exposure and Fyb. For mixed multiport stacks or significant imposed couples outside the published idealization, provide a justified mechanics extension and independent benchmark/test evidence. Do not silently decompose away couples or beneficially redistribute actions.
3. Apply defensible group and geometry adjustments at their correct connection scope, while retaining an independent local bearing/member check. Explain overlap with solved sharing; obtain competent engineering acceptance of any alternative method under NDS11.1.1.3.
4. Check splitting, net tension/shear, row/block tearout, cross-grain tension, washer/pull-through/prying, axial interaction, actual metal design, and loaded-edge/end distances. Passing plastic-yield equations does not exclude brittle member failure.
5. Retain unresolved actual material, glue, screws, supports and finite-motion assumptions. No fabrication or climbing release follows from this audit.
