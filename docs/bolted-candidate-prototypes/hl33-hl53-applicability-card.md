# Simpson HL33 / HL53 applicability card — 2026-09-20

Sources: Simpson's current [C-C-2026 Wood Construction Connectors catalog][catalog],
especially pp. 21–23, 288–289, 315; exact Home Depot listings for [HL33-R][hd33] and
[HL53][hd53]. All dimensions below are nominal catalog dimensions in inches, not a
received-part drill template.

Catalog p. 315, nominal dimensions on **each flange**:

- **HL33 / HL33-R:** 7 ga galvanized; W1 = W2 = 3 1/4, L = 2 1/2;
  D1 = 1 1/4, D2 = —, D3 = 2, D4 = —; one hole per flange. Two 1/2-in bolts total.
  DF/SP allowable uplift (160) = 740 lb; F1 (160) = 1,040 lb per connector.
- **HL53:** 7 ga galvanized; W1 = W2 = 5 3/4, L = 2 1/2;
  D1 = 1 1/4, D2 = —, D3 = 2, D4 = 2 1/2; two holes per flange. Four 1/2-in bolts total.
  DF/SP allowable uplift (160) = 740 lb; F1 (160) = 1,310 lb per connector.

The p. 315 HL drawing and table supply *both* flange widths (W1/W2) and the D1/D3/D4 hole
callouts. Provisional reading: D1 is the 1 1/4-in lengthwise hole-center datum; D3 is the
2-in bend-side row datum; HL53 adds a row at D4 = 2 1/2-in center-to-center. Apply the
catalog drawing to both flanges; do not treat one leg's holes as the whole pattern. Simpson's
[answer on the HL33-R listing][qa33] also states 1 1/4 in from the part edge to the hole
center in both directions. Confirm which physical
edges and bend line register these nominal datums on the *received* brackets before any wood
layout; the catalog supplies no drill jig or fabrication tolerances. [Catalog p. 315][catalog].

Applicability conditions: center each part on a wood member face at least as wide as its
angle; “3” and “5” series wood members need **at least 3 1/2 in thickness** for table loads.
Use the full per-angle through-bolt schedule above: structural-quality bolts at least ASTM
A307 Grade A (not carriage bolts or lag screws), with ASTM A563 Grade A or better nuts.
Simpson allows same-diameter lag screws **at least 5 in long in the beam** as a specific HL
exception, but this card's baseline is the catalog through-bolt schedule. Install specified
fasteners in the correct holes before loading; do not add holes or modify the connector.
[Catalog pp. 21–22, 315][catalog].

Loads are for **one** connector. Simpson permits doubling *uplift* with two connectors and
requires connectors on both sides for lateral loads in both directions; **do not double F1**.
The HL table publishes uplift and F1 only, both labeled **(160)** for wind/earthquake duration
in DF/SP lumber; use the directions depicted in its typical installation, subject to the
actual connection orientation. The [catalog's straps/ties notes, p. 288][catalog] say to reduce
(160) loads where other durations govern; its designer instructions, p. 23, identify ordinary
floor/down duration as (100) and put additional adjustment factors with the designer. This is
an explicit manufacturer route to *reduction*, not a published HL ordinary-duration value or
permission to use the (160) numbers for sustained loads. No HL F2, moment, or combined-load
capacity is asserted here.

Retail identity: Home Depot names **HL33-R** while the current Simpson table names **HL33**;
the [manufacturer's answer on that exact retail listing][qa33] calls the product HL33 and
repeats 740 lb uplift / 1,040 lb F1. That supports applying the HL33 row to the listed item,
but does not define `-R` or prove every future received unit is identical. Home Depot names
the other listing **HL53**, matching the catalog model directly.

**Next gate:** register both flange hole patterns and actual part markings to the received
angles, then have the connection/load path checked against the applicable load duration, wood
species, member geometry and real load directions. This card alone approves no drilling or
structural use.
