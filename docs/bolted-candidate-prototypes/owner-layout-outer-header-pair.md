# Owner layout: outer header/post exception pair

Status: **geometry trial only**. The two original duties are
`clip_timber_header_outer_left/right`. This is not yet an integrated replacement
and does not remove either legacy angle or its SDS axes.

The [bounded screen](../../scripts/owner_layout_outer_header_pair.py) places
mirrored, unnotched 4×6-section blocks on the same inner X sides of the outer
posts as the original brackets. Each block is 139.7 mm inward X by 88.9 mm Y
by 139.7 mm vertical Z, from Y = −150.3 to −61.4 mm and Z = 99.2 to
238.9 mm. It contacts the post side and header underside. Each has two
horizontal post-group bores and two vertical header-group bores through the
full nominal wood grip of 177.8 mm. The paired trial retains the selected
frame's 66 panel/kicker axes and twelve original frame bolts. The one focused
geometry test passes.

These are layout coordinates, not drilling dimensions. The upper post-group
axis is 38.9 mm from the block's top grain end, below a conditional 7D
full-value softwood tension-end marker of 44.45 mm for a 6.35 mm bolt if
that end is loaded. The lower head/tool location is close to the outer floor
runner: a modeled 40 mm tool cylinder centered at Z = 160 mm has only
0.3 mm nominal vertical reserve above the runner's Z = 139.7 mm top.
Those conditions require load-aware placement and an actual tool/sequence
screen; neither is passed here. Complete delivered eight-inch bolt stacks,
washer/nut seats, protected hold/T-nut/LED/wiring volumes, retained hardware,
cost, and joint resistance are also unverified.

No fabrication, purchasing, drilling, or structural release follows. The
pair can enter the complete owner-review assembly only after its remaining
physical interfaces and protected envelopes are reconciled.
