# BR904 Lowe's dimensioned-image follow-up

Status: nominal retail-image geometry for concept screening only; **not** a
controlled shop drawing, received-part measurement, joint rating, or drilling
release. This corrects the earlier claim that no exact-model dimensioned image
could be found online.

[Lowe's exact Adamax BR904 listing](https://www.lowes.com/pd/Adamax-Electro-Galvanized-1-2-in-Angled-Strut-Bracket-with-4-Holes-Strut-Channel-Joining-Accessory/5002818545)
is associated with [this dimensioned product image](https://mobileimages.lowes.com/productimages/d634d853-f9cd-473c-9904-e170613fa268/66922570.jpeg).
The image itself was inspected at 3000 × 1506 pixels. Newhouse says it is
"by Adamax Inc." and separately lists the same BR904 model; this supports,
but does not prove lot-level identity between the two retailers' items.

Read as rounded outer-bend-datum dimensions, the far hole is approximately
`0.81 + 1.87 = 2.68 in` from the free end. Subtracting from the pictured
overall legs gives approximate outer-bend-datum-to-near-hole distances of
`3.48 - 2.68 = 0.80 in` (20.32 mm) on the horizontal leg and
`4.13 - 2.68 = 1.45 in` (36.83 mm) on the vertical leg. These are **derived
from rounded labels**, not direct bend-tangent dimensions or tolerance limits.
They differ substantially from the horizontal 36.5125-mm *hypothetical* offset
used in the earlier fixed-frame screen, so that screen cannot establish actual
BR904 fit.

The image's Ø0.55-in and 1.87-in labels are slightly smaller than Newhouse's
prose 9/16-in hole and 1-7/8-in pitch. This may be rounding, but it cannot be
silently reconciled for bolt-hole tolerance, edge-distance, or drilling checks.
The image lacks a revision, manufacturing tolerances, hole positional limits,
finished minimum thickness, bend radius/tangency, and wood-joint capacity.
The actual factory bracket, bolt, wood bore, washer/nut stack, and complete
joint resistance remain separate gates. Use the image to search a hidden-frame
concept; retain all fixed panel and screw axes; do not order drilling from it.
