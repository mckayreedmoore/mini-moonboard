# PB05 no-pocket nut and wood-washer retail basis

Status (2026-09-21): **retail leads and receiving gate only; no qualified
nut/washer stack, structural acceptance, purchase, or drilling release.** This
independent check covers the twelve 1/4-20 × 8 in outer-upright bolts at the
six PB05 95.25 × 57.15 × 300 mm blocks. The [PB05 no-pocket tolerance
review](simple-pb05-no-pocket-hardware-tolerances.md) models **184.15 mm wood
grip**, one washer under each head and nut, **0.065 in per washer**, **0.226 in
nut height**, and two 1/4-20 pitches (0.100 in) of projection. Those dimensions
are design inputs, not certified retail-part limits. No parts were acquired or
measured; the separate rail-bolt stacks are outside this note.

The 0.734 in washer OD and 0.505 in nut across-corners values in the PB05
model are clearance envelopes, not inspected parts. A flat/USS washer of similar
nominal size is not thereby an NDS standard cut washer; the retail name “cut”
alone is likewise insufficient. Neither route provides a published complete
1/4-in nut–washer stack with SKU-specific limits and a wood-bearing/washer
metal-capacity basis. The Lowe's 5/8 in OD Hillman 490687 SAE washer in an
earlier cost illustration is too small to substitute for PB05's 0.734 in OD
model without a new contact and clearance check.

## Receiving and measurement gate

Before selecting hardware, obtain product-specific washer standard/type and
edition, controlled **ID/OD/thickness ranges**, steel grade/strength, and
evidence that the actual washer is a standard cut washer (or substantiate an
equal-or-greater plate/strap route). Resolve the nut's product grade/proof
load, ASME pattern, thread class, **height range**, bearing face, and tool
envelope. If a seller/manufacturer does not publish those limits, measure and
record the delivered parts with instrument uncertainty; measurement establishes
dimensions, **not** otherwise missing grade or standards certification.

For each received joint (or a justified, traceable lot), identify the exact
nut and two washers; measure washer IDs, ODs, thicknesses, flatness and seating;
nut height, thread engagement and across-corners; finished no-pocket grip at
the actual bore; and bolt underhead length plus first/last **complete** usable
thread. Dry-assemble at limiting grips. With `G` = wood grip, `W_h/W_n` = the
two installed washer thicknesses, `H` = nut height, `S` = first complete
external-thread coordinate, and `E` = last complete external-thread coordinate
from under the head, require at least:

`S_max ≤ W_h,min + G_min + W_n,min` (nut can seat on full thread), and
`E_min ≥ W_h,max + G_max + W_n,max + H_max + 0.100 in` **if** PB05's two-pitch
target means two complete exposed pitches. Also verify physical tip reach and
full effective nut engagement. The [existing tolerance review](simple-pb05-no-pocket-hardware-tolerances.md)
sets out the bolt-length sensitivity; its nominal **7.4676 mm** margin cannot
be claimed as a worst-case reserve with unpublished part and wood ranges.

Finally, assess the actual washer annulus against the **wood bore**, edge
clipping, bearing direction/value and force at each face, plus washer flexure
under the real bolt-head/nut contact and joint action. Passing fit measurements
alone cannot establish NDS washer compliance or structural capacity. Any
failure holds that PB05 operation for documented re-selection/recalculation;
do not omit a washer or add an unstudied pocket to recover bolt reach.
