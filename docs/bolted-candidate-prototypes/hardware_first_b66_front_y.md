# B66/UB66 front-Y center trial: rejected nominal pose

This is one installed-geometry screen. The companion JSON is reproduced by
`scripts/hardware_first_b66_front_y.py --output
docs/bolted-candidate-prototypes/hardware_first_b66_front_y.json`. No
connector, capacity, cut, or drilling decision follows from it.

The kerf-right panel outlines and original 66 screw axes remain fixed.
One solid, front-aligned center post backs both kicker inner edges. A
76.2-mm-thick one-piece rectangular header receives one B66 seat underneath
at the post and two B66 seats atop it at the center principals. The upper
vertical flanges face front Y, at each principal's toe front surface;
their horizontal legs project rearward. The principals are widened to
88.9 mm and butt onto the header. Three factory braces use four 3/8-in
through bolts each. There is no lap joint or custom steel. The 12 old frame
axes remain protected geometry.

The official [MiTek B66 three-view DXF](https://www.mitek-us.com/wp-content/uploads/files/Drawing%20Library/B66_3view.dxf)
gives approximate circle centers 25.4 and 126.744 mm from each leg's free
end. All 12 resulting nominal hole paths, both full plates of each brace,
the changed wood, panels, screws, old frame axes, and neighboring wood are
screened. The approximately 10.3-mm DXF circle and illustrative 2.66-mm
plate envelope are not wood drill sizes or delivered tolerances. The
[drawing follow-up](mitek-b66-drawing-followup.md) documents the DXF and
retail UB66/B66 relationship.

The pose fails at the front face. The principal front surface is at about
Y = **-9.486 mm** at the header toe and moves forward with height. At each
side, the upper vertical plate intersects its principal by **15,279.426
mm³**, and its top seat intersects that principal by **15,445.090 mm³**.
The first vertical bolt path on each side has **5,320.858 mm³** outside
its assigned principal. The second nominal 139.7-mm vertical bore on each
side stops **21.2 mm** short of the far rear face, so it is not a complete
through path. The front flanges are buried in the rising wood rather than
presenting an accessible installed bearing face.

The rectangular one-piece header must reach Y = **-9.486 mm** to support
those seats. Its ideal blank envelope is **2435.225 × 318.614 × 76.2 mm**.
It intersects both fixed kickers by **846,897.255 mm³** each, both fixed
lower panels by about **519,117–520,843 mm³**, and both side members by
**508,698.636 mm³** each. Each widened principal also intersects its
bottom and service rails by **135,193.278 mm³** per rail. The upper
vertical plates intersect the lower panels and one occupied panel screw
path on each side. The second upper vertical bore path intersects that
same protected screw path; each upper second seat bore crosses its kicker
and lower panel. These are direct conflicts with the fixed panel/screw
geometry, even though all 66 original screw cylinders still intersect
their assigned receiving timber.

No distinct brace plates intersect each other, and the nominal bores do
not intersect each other or the 12 old frame axes. Those clearances do not
resolve the wood and panel collisions. The lower post's ideal blank is
184.15 × 139.7 × 238.9 mm; the one-piece header and post's grade and
availability are unverified. Actual head, washer, nut, grip, wrench sweep,
and removal with panels installed are not established. The buried front
flanges already prevent an ordinary installed access claim.

[ESR-3455](https://www.mitek-us.com/wp-content/uploads/files/pdf/Code%20Evaluation%20Reports/esrESR-3455.pdf)
requires at least 3 in. actual receiving-member thickness and four 3/8-in
ASTM A307 Grade A or better bolts for B66. The nominal stock envelopes
reach that thickness floor, but the complete upper bore support fails.
Its B66 F1/F2 table is for `C_D = 1.6` and cannot be converted to ordinary
duration. Those load axes and paired-brace conditions do not rate this
center joint's combined force, moment, or reversal. Ordinary-duration
resistance needs separate analysis of wood, bolts, actual load path, and
the formed ASTM A653 Structural Steel Grade 40 bracket with at least
0.099-in base-steel thickness. No F1/F2 value is assigned here.

This is the end of this single pose. No drilling or rating approval.
