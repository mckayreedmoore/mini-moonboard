# Rear Y-face HL35 center pose — rejected

One kerf-right installed-geometry diagnostic. The paired JSON is generated by
`uv run python -m scripts.hardware_first_center_rear_yface --output
docs/bolted-candidate-prototypes/hardware_first_center_rear_yface.json`.
This is neither a drilling plan nor a structural claim.

The hypothetical solid common post spans X = −92.075..92.075,
Y = −175.7..−36, Z = 0..238.9 mm. Its front is flush with both kicker backs,
and it covers the kerf-right seam at X = −1.5875 mm. One HL35 is centered on
the **rear Y face only**, with its seat under a solid 88.9-mm-deep header.
Two more rear Y-face HL35 envelopes attempt to connect the original left and
right principals above that header. The header spans Y = −265.25..−36 and
Z = 238.9..327.8 mm; the original principal toes are trimmed to its top.
Butt contact is the only wood contact assumed. There is no lap or custom steel.

The nominal [Simpson HL drawing](https://dhcsupplies.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/Documents/simpson-strong-tie/hl-angles.pdf)
supports a 127-mm bend length, 82.55-mm leg reach, and four 1/2-in bolts per
HL35. Rectangular 4.55-mm plate envelopes and 14.2875-mm wood bore cylinders
are illustrative. The horizontal hole offset is assumed by symmetry, not
dimensioned as a factory drilling coordinate. Delivered bend, holes, coating,
washers, and tolerances are absent from the model.

All 66 selected panel/kicker axes remain fixed. The four center kicker
occupied cylinders each intersect the new solid post by 438.127 mm³; this
establishes nominal receiving wood, not screw embedment or edge capacity.
No ideal angle plate intersects a panel, protected screw cylinder, or other
modeled timber. Toe trimming loses no modeled principal screw/receiver
intersection. The new header does intersect each unchanged side timber by
629,301 mm³. Without toe trimming it intersects each old principal by
283,200 mm³. The two upper vertical plates have a 1.4635-mm gap to their
trimmed principal at the bottom of their span, so the ideal rectangles do
not seat on those sloped faces. No separate angle-to-angle overlap occurs.

Both nominal lower post bores and all six nominal header bores lie in their
intended new wood. Each existing principal is only 38.1 mm wide in X, while
the upper HL35's two nominal bolt axes are at X = −101.75/−38.25 or
+38.25/+101.75; all four upper principal bore cylinders miss their intended
timbers entirely (22,397 mm³ outside wood apiece). Two independent
upper/lower header bore pairs overlap by 3,097 mm³ each. No nominal bore
intersects a protected screw occupied cylinder. None of the 12 existing
frame-bolt occupied cylinders intersects a new plate or bore. These are
conditional
occupied-cylinder screens, not bolt or hole instructions.

**Disposition: reject this pose.** The upper bracket cannot attach to the
trimmed principal at this rear reference face, the independent header bolts
cross, and the header conflicts with both adjacent side timbers. Bolt head,
nut, washer, tool, and withdrawal access has not been established at either
level. The butt-contact post/header/principal load path and all connector
resistance remain unqualified. No cutting, drilling, procurement, or load
rating follows from this trial.
