# Outward-post barrel/backer MVP: retail cost screen

Checked **21 September 2026 (America/Denver)**. USD, public US Lowe's and Home
Depot listings only. No seller contact, cart, or purchase. This prices the
**active kerf-right outward-post viewer**, not the selected floor-flush build.
It is a material inventory and partial retail cost, **not a complete dollar
quote**: several required items have no verifiable price or selected retail
part. No joint, drilling, fabrication, or climbing release follows from it.

## Result and counting rule

- **$108.92 observable bolt-and-barrel basket**: all 48 former-angle duty
  bolts and barrels **plus** all four header-to-backer bolts and barrels.
  This is 52 nominal pairs, not 48, and includes no washers or retained frame
  hardware. The exact length-mix arithmetic is below.
- **$15.96 observable replacement value** for two 50-count boxes of the 66
  retained, owner-purchased panel/kicker screws. Fresh cash is **$0 only if**
  at least 66 suitable screws remain on hand. With replacement, the priced
  subset becomes **$124.88**. Neither number is a project total.
- **No finite complete retail range is supportable.** Required washers,
  retained Grade 5 stacks, lumber, plywood value/shortage, hold hardware,
  pads, and consumables have unresolved price or inventory. The known-price
  subset is $108.92–$124.88 across the two screw-inventory scenarios;
  if these exact trial leads prove usable, the complete checkout adds unknown
  nonnegative amounts, with no defensible upper bound. If they do not prove
  usable, even this subset must be repriced. A $7.92–$15.75 *washer catalog
  comparison* below
  is deliberately outside these subtotals because fit is not established.

"Price" here means the amount displayed for the exact retailer listing during
this check, not a Denver store quote, assured stock, delivered cost, or approval
of its mechanical properties. Some Home Depot prices surfaced in its public
product/search rendering while the direct page omitted a price without a store.
Lowe's likewise may ask for location. Recheck exact SKU, package, store and
price before any later purchasing decision.

## How to finish the estimate later

The complete first-checkout equation is **$108.92 + compatible 52-washer
basket + any 42605 shortfall + four 4×6 sticks + nine 2×6 sticks + any
backer-stock shortfall + any plywood shortfall/value basis + retained
12-bolt/12-nut/24-washer stack shortfall + hold/pad/finish/tool/freight
requirements**, with tax and contingency separately if wanted. Each unknown
must be filled from a current exact Lowe's/Home Depot listing or marked owned
after a physical count. Until then, quoting a single "complete MVP cost" or a
closed dollar interval would invent prices or compatibility.
