# Paired outer-post HL33 lower-joint trial

This is one **rejected** lower-only, kerf-right installed-geometry screen. The
paired JSON is generated by the matching script with `--output` pointing to
the JSON file in this folder.
It is an occupied-volume diagnostic, not a drilling plan or a connector rating.
The upper joint is outside this assignment.

The Simpson C-C-2026 catalog (HL33 row, PDF p. 315)
lists HL33 with 82.55-mm legs, a 63.5-mm bend length, one 12.7-mm bolt per
leg, 31.75-mm along-bend hole offset and 50.8-mm vertical-leg hole offset.
The catalog detail requires at least 88.9 mm of wood at **both** bolted
members. It does not dimension the horizontal-flange hole distance from the
bend; this trial assumes 50.8 mm. Ideal plate thickness 4.55 mm and occupied
wood bore diameter 14.2875 mm are also diagnostic assumptions.

One common post is centered at X = 0 with its front at Y = -36 mm, aligned
with both kicker rear edges. Its section is the specified 190.5 × 90.47 mm,
so its rear is Y = -126.47 mm. It stops at Z = 234.35 mm, leaving the assumed
4.55-mm seat space under the header. The one-piece header retains the original
full-span shape and has a fused, 460-mm-wide raised central region from
Z = 238.9 to 327.8 mm. Thus both bolt receivers meet the nominal 88.9-mm
catalog minimum: 190.5 mm across the post and 88.9 mm through the raised
header center. The post/header contact and bracket force path remain to be
established; the seats occupy only part of their nominal gap. At the kicker
seam, their 25.4-mm center separation leaves a 4.55-mm-high band with no
continuous rear wood support. This independently rejects the modeled shape
despite its retained screw receiving volumes.

The two HL33 vertical legs bear on the post's outer X faces. A full 63.5-mm
leg can nominally start anywhere from Y = -126.47 to -99.5 mm while staying
on the post, assuming the seat remains supported by the header. This trial
tests only the rear-flush start at Y = -126.47 mm; it reaches 82.55 mm down
from the seat, to Z = 151.8 mm.
Their single post holes coincide at Y = -94.72, Z = 183.55 mm. The paired
angles therefore require **one shared full-width post bolt axis**, not two
independent post bolts. Their inward seats stop 25.4 mm apart. The two
independent header hole axes are at X = ±44.45 mm, Y = -94.72 mm, with
88.9 mm transverse separation. These are assumed nominal coordinates used
only to screen full bore volumes. The assumed seat elevation is Z = 234.35
to 238.9 mm; shifting the bracket along Y cannot cure the header's broad
principal overlap.

**Reject this installed pose.** The raised 460-mm-wide header intersects
each unchanged center principal by 269,700.459 mm³. Each full nominal header
bore also enters its adjacent principal by 130.436 mm³. The two independent
header bores do not cross each other or exit the intended header, and no plate
intersects panel, intended timber, or adjacent timber. The post and header
are each one CAD solid. The catalog minimum and example stock dimensions
pass, but those passes cannot cure the two installed collisions or seam gap. This trial
does not reshape principals or specify any upper-joint connection.

The JSON records intersections of full plate and bore solids with panels,
frozen screws, existing frame axes, and neighboring timber. It compares all
66 screw receiver volumes before and after the post/header change, and also
checks the four center kicker screws against the new post. All 66 frozen
screw axes retain their modeled receivers; the four center kicker screws
each intersect the post. No frozen 50.8-mm screw envelope, 63.5-mm overall
screw sensitivity envelope, or existing frame-bolt axis intersects the new
hardware. The longer envelope treats purchased **overall** screw length as
occupied; it is a conservative sensitivity, not a measured shaft envelope.

An illustrative 25.4-mm washer outside diameter leaves 63.5 mm nominal
clear edge spacing between header washers at the same Y coordinate. At the
shared post bolt, the nominal radial disc leaves 19.05 mm to the rear edge
and 46.02 mm to the front edge; the generated JSON supplies these values.
These disc comparisons do not establish head, nut, washer thickness, bearing,
grip, socket/wrench access, withdrawal space, or a valid shared-bolt detail.

The post's axis-aligned blank envelope is 190.5 × 90.47 × 234.35 mm. The
specified Lowe's 4×8×8-ft model 110341 (source URL in the JSON)
lists a 190.5 × 90.47-mm section and dimensionally contains that blank.
As an optional dry-stock lead, Lowe's item 100565 / model 151980
lists #2 Better kiln-dried Douglas fir, 3.562 × 7.5 in actual, and no
"no longer sold" notice on the opened page; local stock is unknown.
Either listed thickness exceeds the 88.9-mm HL minimum by only 1.57 mm.
The shaped header is dimensionally contained by the listed 90.47 × 241.3 ×
3657.6-mm Lowe's 4×10×12-ft #2 Better Douglas-fir green comparator (source
URL in the JSON). Local stock, delivered dry size, straightness, usable yield,
and machining allowance are unknown. The header's modeled full-span length
leaves only 3.175 mm total surplus from an 8-ft blank, so that is not a
practical cutting allowance.

The bracketed members and frozen panel/kicker axes are preserved only as
ideal CAD geometry. Delivered connector hole and bend geometry, bolt stacks,
installation access, edge/end distance, paired-angle action, wood and steel
resistance, and the complete reversible load path require separate review.
No rating, purchase, cut, or drilling instruction follows from this trial.
