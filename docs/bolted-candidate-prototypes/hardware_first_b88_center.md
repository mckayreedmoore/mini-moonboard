# MiTek B88 / Home Depot UB88 center geometry trial

This is one bounded nominal installed-geometry diagnostic for the separate
bolted candidate. It is not a capacity verdict, connector selection, purchase
instruction, or drilling release. The companion JSON is generated by
`uv run python -m scripts.hardware_first_b88_center --output
docs/bolted-candidate-prototypes/hardware_first_b88_center.json`.

The model uses the six factory DXF circle centers documented in
`mitek-b88-drawing-followup.md`: horizontal offsets of 28.6, 92.2, and
155.8 mm and vertical offsets of 29.0, 92.6, and 156.2 mm, each measured
from its free end. The two sets differ by about 0.4 mm. The modeled circle
diameter is 10.3 mm. These are nominal CAD observations, not tolerance or
wood-hole instructions. The illustrative plate thickness is 2.66 mm; the
actual bend, coating, and delivered-part variation are not modeled.

The single layout replaces the two lower center posts with one conceptual
solid common receiver, widens the two upper center principals to conceptual
3.5-in actual width, and increases the header depth to the B88 table's
3-in minimum. Four B88 braces sit on the outer X faces of the lower and
upper center receivers. Left and right braces are separated across the
header depth. This is bracket hardware; there are no lap joints or custom
steel. Butt contact is allowed, and the fastener concept is removable
through-bolts. The selected panel face, kerf-right option, and all 66 panel/kicker
screw axes are unchanged. The common lower receiver touches both fixed
kicker inner edges and intersects all 66 frozen screw occupancy cylinders
at their respective receiving members. That intersection is a nominal CAD
check, not a Hillman embedment or edge-support qualification.

At this pose, the 24 nominal B88 circle paths have four direct conflicts
with protected kicker screw occupied cylinders. Four paths on the upper
vertical legs leave the modeled oblique principal wood. The widened header
intersects both existing side members; the widened principals intersect
three existing half-rails per side; one upper vertical plate intersects a
bottom half-rail. These are installed-fit blockers requiring a different
hidden-frame design or bracket pose. The plate rectangles do not overlap
the protected panels, protected screw cylinders, or one another in this
idealized screen. The JSON names each conflict without publishing installed
drill locations.

Full bolt heads, washers, nuts, grip, tool sweep, access, and disassembly
are unmodeled. Nominal 3-in receiver thickness alone does not establish
adequate edge/end distances, full bearing, wood or steel resistance, or
load-direction applicability. Existing frame bolts and neighboring joints
would need rechecking after any hidden-member change. No manufacturing
tolerance source or delivered UB88 measurement has been checked. This exact
pose is blocked on geometry. ESR-3455 expressly prohibits adjusting the
published `C_D = 1.6` F1/F2 values to ordinary duration; none is treated as
a MoonBoard capacity. The B88 is neither selected nor rated here. No cutting
or drilling follows from this diagnostic.
