# Owner barrel-nut retail qualification update

21 September 2026. Public-source research only. This extends, without repeating
its exact SKUs, the [bounded PB01 search](simple-pb01-cross-dowel-qualified-retail-search.md).
Gate: **one identifiable Lowe's or Home Depot cross dowel** needs a
manufacturer-authored controlled OD, length, thread-axis location, usable
thread span and tolerances **and** a traceable minimum material/thread
resistance or part-specific test. A nominal retail size or a generic bolt
grade cannot establish the complete barrel or wood-joint resistance.

It supplies no corresponding standard, certificate, component assignment, cross-hole drawing, or tested load. | **No.** “Grade 5” cannot be assigned to the barrel thread or used as its minimum strength without traceable factory evidence; geometry is also insufficient. |

Search also considered published cross-dowel testing, including a [furniture
joint experiment][joint-test]. Its joint result does not identify or qualify
any listed retail nut. Generic material tables and another maker's drawings
likewise do not transfer to a retail SKU. The closest new nominal axis datum
is the dual-hole marketplace listing, but it is **not** a controlled
manufacturing dimension.

**Finding: no new candidate clears both gates in the public sources located.**
This is a bounded documentation result, not evidence that a cross dowel would
fail. Keep the barrel design as a trial only; do not select a drilling size,
borrow a grade, or infer an installed-joint rating from these listings. A
later public manufacturer print plus traceable material/thread minimum or
part-specific test would change this finding for its exact retail identity.
