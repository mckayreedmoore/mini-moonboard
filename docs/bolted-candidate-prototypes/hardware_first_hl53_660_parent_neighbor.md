reconstructed parent context; the assembly and joint remain unqualified.**

prototype, not fabrication or drilling data.

The screen carries forward the kerf-right panels and all 66 fixed
panel/kicker screw axes. It places the earlier one-piece, zero-gap

the parent header, central post, front carriers, ribs and rib posts, the

the eight nominal HL53 header brackets with 32 ideal 12.7-mm bolt paths,
64 ideal washers, and 64 short straight tool cylinders. The rail joint
has four ideal bolts, eight washers, and eight short tool cylinders.

At the modeled tolerance of 0.01 mm³, the new rail and its nominal
bracket/bolt/washer/tool envelopes do not overlap the parent wood, the
eight header brackets or their bolt/washer/tool envelopes, panels, or
fixed panel screws. The new rail does not remove any modeled receiving
wood from the 66 fixed screw axes. The 12 original frame-bolt axes also
show no modeled overlap with this one rail joint, but their applicability
to a changed frame remains unproved and they are not adopted as a new
connection design. The earlier isolated zero-gap result still supplies
the local seat-contact and four-hole/wood-receiver observations; this
screen adds only parent-neighbor interference, not a new strength check.
