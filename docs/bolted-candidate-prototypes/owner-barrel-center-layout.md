# Direct cross-dowel center-six viewer concept (Track B)

This source-distinct viewer trial covers the four center/header duties
`clip_split_header_center_left/right` and `clip_split_base_center_left/right`,
plus the two bottom-center duties `clip_horizontal_bottom_left_2/right_1`.
It uses original-width center posts placed at X = ±180 mm and the separate
kerf-right kicker-screw backers. It adds no PB02 rear-return chain, corner
blocks, or legacy brackets. The original whole 2×6/header/rail/principal
butts, 66 panel/kicker screw axes, and 12 frame bolts are protected.

The script imports the cross-dowel continuation's provisional 0.630 in
barrel length, 0.394 in OD, 1/4-20 thread major, 5 in bolt length, and
0.065 in washer-thickness sensitivity. It uses **one assumed 8.001 mm**
barrel-end-to-thread-axis offset, centered in the 16.002 mm nominal body.
The 6–10 mm offset sensitivity remains open. The provisional bore equals
the barrel nominal OD, with *no controlled drilling clearance*. The 7.5 mm
bolt bore, 25.4 mm washer OD, 11 mm head, and 20 mm tool diameter are
diagnostic envelopes, not verified purchased dimensions. Hillman 880543
retail identity at Lowe's 3012559 and Home Depot 202242356 / 933902 is
not a controlled thread-axis print, delivered-length measurement, fit,
material grade, or resistance basis. No purchased Hillman fit verdict.

The two post/header joints place vertical bolts from the header top through
the 38.1 mm header into the posts, crossing X-axis barrels in the posts at
Z = 200 mm and Y = −145/−95 mm. The two principal/header joints place
vertical bolts from the header underside into the principals, crossing
X-axis barrels at Z = 305 mm. Their Y rows are −163/−137.6 mm. A 25.4 mm
washer at the rear row meets the header rear edge with **zero nominal Y
margin**; the forward row meets the fixed kicker backer's rear Y edge with
**zero nominal margin**. These principal/header stations require a compact
explicit alternate or verified revised hardware/pose, not a tolerance-free
selection.

The two bottom-center joints use +/−X bolts from each principal outside
face through its 38.1 mm thickness into the same-side rail butt. The rail
barrel centers sit 70 mm into the rail at local N = 250/310 mm, with
T = rail rear T + 19.05 mm. Barrel cross bores enter along +T. The rows are
deliberately away from the known left E1–E2 rail wiring passage, but the
full nominal 5 in bolt shafts, all installed solid envelopes, and the
retained wire route must be checked before even a viewer fit claim.

The model requires positive butt contact, full intended core wood paths,
and a positive *intersecting bolt/barrel bore* volume for each of 12 poses.
It screens shafts, bores, bodies, heads, washers, and both tool directions
against 142 T-nuts, 142 provisional 50.8 mm rear hold paths, 132 LED
bodies, 131 wires, all 66 panel screws, 12 frame bolts, and unrelated
timber/backers. A mathematical hole intersection is not proof of thread
engagement, adequate metal, wood strength, installability, or load path.
The source reports each incomplete core, protected hit, unrelated-wood hit,
and station requiring an alternate; it does not silently accept them.

The `build_layout(wood)` viewer entry point supplies six station rows with
provisional full-length bolt axes, barrel solids, shaft/washer/head stacks,
drilling envelopes, and tool paths. The focused CAD suite found no finite
protected-service hits on those direct paths. Both bottom-center and both
post/header stations remain direct viewer-only poses. The two
principal/header stations are **REVISE** because their nominal washer
edge/backer margin is zero. Those two rows carry a small explicit side-block
solid as a visual compact-alternate placeholder. Its screened protected and
wood overlaps are empty, but it has no fasteners, no verified contact/load
path, and is **not** a corrected joint. The shown direct bolts do not fasten
it. Whole uncut timber is used for this new topology; legacy SDS cuts in the
historical finished parts must not be treated as current bore voids.

This is a viewer-development concept only. Barrel material and internal
thread stripping, bolt grade/threads/length, washer bearing, barrel bearing,
splitting, end tear-out, net section, group action, sequential drilling,
barrel insertion/removal, and tool clearance remain unresolved. No native
solve, drilling, fabrication, or structural release follows.
