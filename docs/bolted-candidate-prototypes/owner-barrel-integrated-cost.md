release**. The bolt mix comes from the published integrated viewer, which has
46 new barrel pairs, **zero** separate kicker backers, 66 retained panel/kicker
screws and 12 retained frame-bolt axes. The displayed bolt/barrel prices were
rechecked on public Lowe's/Home Depot pages on **22 September 2026**, but
**not for a selected store**. The historical 4½ in lead's thread span is
still unknown. Fit,
grade, usable thread and stock are not established by a price listing. The
centered principal/header revision removes both earlier 4½ in trial bolts
and their two barrel/washer pairs; other families are unchanged.

This size-only subset is **not currently one confirmed fillable order**:
[Lowe's Hillman 880543](https://www.lowes.com/pd/Hillman-20-x-5-8-in-Slotted-Drive-Zinc-plated-Barrel-Nut/3012559)
showed 36 available for shipping and 21 for delivery in the default page
context, with pickup unavailable. Neither channel alone covers 46, and their
quantities cannot simply be added. A different store or lot could change
availability. An [Everbilt 801914 1/4-20 four-pack](https://www.homedepot.com/p/204276112)
is $5.98; twelve packs would supply 48 for $71.76, but its controlled barrel
diameter, axis location, thread depth and resistance are not established as
interchangeable with the modeled Hillman body.

The scene calls for **46 head-side washers**, including two modeled 19.05 mm
outer-diameter envelopes at the revised center principal/header joint. None
is yet an identified, dimension-controlled, strength-qualified retail part.
The earlier [washer comparison](owner-barrel-mvp-retail-cost.md) is not a
compatible-price bound. The two 4½ in bolts belong only to the historical
two-row trial. The $97.28 basket is a catalog
scale, not an installable or complete checkout package.

For an **unqualified size-only checkout comparison**, one
[Home Depot Everbilt 800452 100-pack of 1/4-in washers](https://www.homedepot.com/p/204276405)
is $7.97, bringing the Hillman-barrel/bolt/washer arithmetic to **$105.25**.
Alternatively, 46 individually listed
[Lowe's Hillman 811070 1/4-in washers](https://www.lowes.com/pd/Hillman-1-4-in-Hot-Dipped-Galvanized-Standard-SAE-Flat-Washer/3037537)
at $0.26 each and twelve Everbilt barrel packs yield **$112.92**. These are
two *catalog comparisons*, **not** lower/upper bounds for a compatible or
obtainable 46-pair set. Washer OD, thickness, ID tolerance, bearing capacity,
wood seat, finish compatibility and delivered fit remain unverified; the
19.05 mm trial center seats have no established tolerance allowance. The
12 retained Grade 5 frame-bolt stacks, wood and plywood are outside both
figures. Do not purchase on this comparison.

Two modeled 88.9 × 139.7 × 238.9 mm center posts replace the prior narrow
center posts and two separate backers. In principle, one post could be cut
from each of the two 4×6 leg-stock offcuts; the
[stock-lead screen](owner-barrel-center-post-retail.md) gives the arithmetic.
This is **not a $0 stock claim**: the public 4×6 listings are green and larger
than the modeled section, and delivered pieces, grade/moisture, saw kerf,
defects, trimming and offcut allocations must be verified. Baseline full-stick
allowance remains two 4×6×10 ft, two 4×6×8 ft, three 2×6×10 ft and six
2×6×8 ft, all unpriced on an exact delivered-stock basis. The kerf-right
kicker panels may need another plywood sheet/offcut after two 4×8 main-panel
sheets; quantity and price remain open. **Do not yet assume that the two owned
sheets yield the four main panels.** The
[Lowe's Roseburg sheet listing](https://www.lowes.com/pd/Roseburg-23-32-CAT-PS1-09-Square-Structural-Plywood-Douglas-Fir-Application-as-4-x-8/1000015973)
shows an **actual width of 3.953 ft (47.436 in)** and **actual length of
7.989 ft (95.868 in)**. Each rectangular
[kerf-right main blank](../floor-flush-construction-kerf-right/stock.csv)
requires **47.9375 × 48 in**. Two widths plus the assumed 1/8-in kerf use
the full 96-in length exactly; the listing's stated length is already
**0.132 in short**, and its stated width is **0.564 in short** of the 48-in
blank height. The page also lists an internally inconsistent 3.99-ft minimum
width and 7.99-ft minimum length, so its dimensions are not a verified
measurement of the owner's sheets. If its actual dimensions apply, the
rectangular main-blank plan fails before kicker allocation: **a third sheet
reserved for kickers does not repair that main-panel yield**. Roseburg's
[AC sanded brochure](https://www.roseburg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SandedPlywood_Brochure.pdf)
calls the product 4 × 8 ft but does not establish the owned sheet's actual
width. This is a measurement and profile-nesting gate, not a finding that the
owner's sheets are wrong; do not change fixed panel geometry to match an
unmeasured listing. Measure both owned sheets in both directions and verify
an actual profile cut layout before setting the plywood purchase count.
Retained frame stacks, hold/LED/pad
shortfalls, finishing, tools, delivery, tax and labor are likewise outside
the $97.28 subset.

Do not quote $97.28 as an MVP total or treat these catalog leads as qualified
hardware. A defensible complete low/high range is not available: exact
store-specific timber, plywood, washer, retained-bolt and other item prices,
quantities or fit remain open. A complete estimate requires an exact
store/stock check and an accepted geometry/material package first; no
drilling, fabrication or climbing
is authorized by this cost screen.
