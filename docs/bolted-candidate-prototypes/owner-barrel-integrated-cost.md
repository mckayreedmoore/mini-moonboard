# Integrated center-post barrel candidate: bounded cost snapshot

This is a **partial USD catalog screen, not a complete project quote or purchase
release**. The bolt mix comes from the published integrated viewer, which has
46 new barrel pairs, **zero** separate kicker backers, 66 retained panel/kicker
screws and 12 retained frame-bolt axes. Prices below were observed on public
Lowe's/Home Depot pages for the earlier 21 September 2026 screen and have **not
been rechecked for a selected store**. The 4½ in lead was found in a 22
September public retailer search; its thread span is still unknown. Fit,
grade, usable thread and stock are not established by a price listing. The
centered principal/header revision removes both earlier 4½ in trial bolts
and their two barrel/washer pairs; other families are unchanged.

The scene calls for **46 head-side washers**, including two modeled 19.05 mm
outer-diameter envelopes at the revised center principal/header joint. None
is yet an identified, dimension-controlled, strength-qualified retail part.
The earlier [washer comparison](owner-barrel-mvp-retail-cost.md) is not a
compatible-price bound. The two 4½ in bolts belong only to the historical
two-row trial. The $97.28 basket is a catalog
scale, not an installable or complete checkout package.

Two modeled 88.9 × 139.7 × 238.9 mm center posts replace the prior narrow
center posts and two separate backers. In principle, one post could be cut
from each of the two 4×6 leg-stock offcuts; the
[stock-lead screen](owner-barrel-center-post-retail.md) gives the arithmetic.
This is **not a $0 stock claim**: the public 4×6 listings are green and larger
than the modeled section, and delivered pieces, grade/moisture, saw kerf,
defects, trimming and offcut allocations must be verified. Baseline full-stick
allowance remains two 4×6×10 ft, two 4×6×8 ft, three 2×6×10 ft and six
2×6×8 ft, all unpriced on an exact delivered-stock basis. The kerf-right
kicker panels may need another plywood sheet/offcut after two 4×8 main-panel
sheets; quantity and price remain open. The
[Lowe's Roseburg sheet listing](https://www.lowes.com/pd/Roseburg-23-32-CAT-PS1-09-Square-Structural-Plywood-Douglas-Fir-Application-as-4-x-8/1000015973)
shows an **actual width of 3.953 ft (47.436 in)**, while each
[kerf-right blank](../floor-flush-construction-kerf-right/stock.csv)
requires 1217.6125 mm (47.9375 in). If that listing describes the owned
sheets, they are about **0.5015 in too narrow before cutting**. Roseburg's
[AC sanded brochure](https://www.roseburg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/SandedPlywood_Brochure.pdf)
calls the product 4 × 8 ft but does not establish the owned sheet's actual
width. This is a measurement gate, not a finding that the user's sheets are
wrong; do not change fixed panel geometry to match an unmeasured listing.
Retained frame stacks, hold/LED/pad
shortfalls, finishing, tools, delivery, tax and labor are likewise outside
the $97.28 subset.

Do not quote $97.28 as an MVP total or treat these catalog leads as qualified
hardware. A defensible complete low/high range is not available: exact
store-specific timber, plywood, washer, retained-bolt and other item prices,
quantities or fit remain open. A complete estimate requires an exact
store/stock check and an accepted geometry/material package first; no
drilling, fabrication or climbing
is authorized by this cost screen.
