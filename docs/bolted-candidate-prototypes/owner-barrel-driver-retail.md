# 7/16-in center-joint driver: ordinary-retail diameter evidence

Public-source check, 2026-09-22. This is a tool-geometry lead, not a changed CAD
envelope, purchased-tool inspection, or fabrication release.

**Verified size lead:** URREA **4714**, 1/4-in-drive, 6-point 7/16-in short
socket, is [Home Depot Internet #202796499](https://www.homedepot.com/p/202796499).
[URREA's own socket table, p. 73](https://medios.urrea.com/catalogo/ListasDePrecios/UPT/2023/URR/UPTU22_73.pdf)
explicitly lists **nut-end outside diameter 5/8 in (15.875 mm)**, drive-end
outside diameter also 5/8 in, overall length 1 in (25.4 mm), and nut depth
7/16 in (11.1125 mm). The row identifies **4714**, not just the separately
listed 4714T. Home Depot shows **$1.52**; local stock and delivery to the build
location were not verified.

Thus one named, non-specialty Home Depot socket has a published *nominal*
working-end OD below 18 mm; the 7/16-in opening is the bolt-head across-flats
size, **not** the outside diameter. URREA gives no controlled maximum OD,
manufacturing tolerance, or dimensioned profile between its two ends in that
table. The socket's 5/8-in drive-end figure does not specify the OD of an
attached extension, ratchet, or hand driver. Its 7/16-in nut depth does not by
itself prove engagement with the delivered bolt head or clearance for the bolt
and pocket stack.

Other ordinary listings checked: [Lowe's CRAFTSMAN CMMT43498, item
#1048948](https://www.lowes.com/pd/CRAFTSMAN-Standard-SAE-1-4-in-Drive-6-point-7-16-in-Shallow-Socket/1000596557)
and [Home Depot TEKTON SHD02011, Internet
#310621256](https://www.homedepot.com/p/310621256) identify 1/4-in-drive
7/16-in shallow sockets, but their accessible retailer specifications do not
publish a working-end OD. The [TEKTON product
page](https://www.tekton.com/1-4-inch-drive-x-7-16-inch-shallow-6-point-socket-shd02011)
links a specifications image; its accessible text does not establish a
working-end OD. Home Depot's [Klein 630-7/16 nut driver, Internet
#202282839](https://www.homedepot.com/p/202282839) lists a 3-in hollow shaft,
but no verified working-end OD was found. None of these are accepted as an
additional ≤18 mm candidate from the checked evidence.

**Implication:** URREA 4714 justifies testing a smaller *socket-only* nominal
envelope than the current 20 mm diagnostic driver, but does **not** justify
changing that full swept-tool envelope yet. Check the actual socket and
extension/handle geometry, straight and angled reach into the recessed seat,
and delivered head fit; retain 20 mm in CAD until the complete access path is
verified. No purchase or contact was made.
