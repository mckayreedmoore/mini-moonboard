# PB06 top-single pair: detached full-section trial

Decision: **REVISE**. This screen does not replace either live ML24Z/SDS duty
or authorize cutting, drilling, or hardware purchase.

The exact PB06 stations are `clip_single_top_left_1` and
`clip_single_top_right_2`: the top 2×6 rail butts against the solid outer 4×6
sides. The trial reuses the small no-pocket block family: two independent
95.25 × 57.15 × 300 mm solid-timber blocks, with two 1/4-in through-bolts
into each side and two into the top rail. Its not-yet-selected nominal stock
envelopes are four 8-in and four 5-in bolts, eight nuts, and sixteen washers.
The proposed topology uses no lap or custom steel and would keep members
individually demountable only if a physically seated version can be found.

The [reproducible screen](../../scripts/simple_pb06_top_single_pair.py) checks
the actual PB06 solids, full nominal shafts, bores, washers/nuts, temporary
40-mm tool envelopes, retained angles/axes, and signed member edges. Both
rail/block contacts and the four rail bores are present. But the top of each
side is cut away at this pose: side/block contact is **0 mm²** and the four
side bores are incomplete. The minimum signed edge margin is **−28.575 mm**
versus a conditional 4D = **25.4 mm** target. No neighboring protected solid
or axis collision was reported; that alone is not a feasible joint. A cleat
on the opposite rail face or a lower pose would need a new complete screen.

PB06 still has its ten existing blocks, 12 original frame bolts, all 66
panel/kicker axes, and 12 legacy duties (72 SDS axes), including the two
target duties. No load transfer, full wood/bolt joint checks, exact purchased
hardware, tolerance fit, tool procedure, or structural release is established.
