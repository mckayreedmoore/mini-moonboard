**Offcut arithmetic, not an inventory claim.** The schedule gives each leg a
2028.463 mm 4×6 blank allowance. An 8 ft stick is 2438.4 mm, leaving
409.937 mm *before kerf, end trim, and defects* per leg. At an illustrative
3.2 mm crosscut kerf, one 238.9 mm post from **each** leg stick leaves about
164.6 mm after the leg and post crosscuts. Two post lengths cannot come from
one such offcut (2 × 238.9 + 3.2 = 481 mm). The leg blanks themselves cannot
be shortened to make posts. A 4×4 post would also need a lengthwise rip from
the 4×6 offcut; its remaining width cannot yield another 88.9 mm-wide post.
The [earlier cost screen](owner-barrel-mvp-retail-cost.md) tentatively assigned
these same two leg offcuts to separate kicker backers. That allocation is
superseded by the owner's center-post replacement direction; do not double-count
it as a current need. The actual purchased leg length, dry/grade stamps, cut plan,
defects, end-trim allowance, saw kerf, and whether offcuts remain unallocated
are all unverified. Extra post-stock cost is therefore **unknown**, not $0.

For a fast physical check, first confirm the actual two leg sticks and
measure the cuttable offcuts, section, moisture/stamp,
and defects. If they fail, the Home Depot kiln-dried 4×4 is a separate short
stock lead; a dry 4×6 of the modeled section has **not** been established by
these listings. Retail “#2,” “Premium,” or “structural” wording supplies no
center-joint capacity. Species group/grade stamp, delivered dimensions,
moisture, bore and fastener edges, protected geometry, load path, and joint
resistance remain open.
