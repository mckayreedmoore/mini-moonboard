# Profiled bottom 4×6 center-rail relief

**Status: nominal CAD fit only; no connection qualification.** This bounded
sidecar starts from the rejected six-rail thickening trial. Only the left and
right *bottom* cut rails change. The four service rails and the tongue-concept
post, header, principals, ideal upper HL33 seats, and bore envelopes retain
their prior geometry. The paired JSON is generated by:

`uv run python -m scripts.hardware_first_center_rail_relief \
  --output docs/bolted-candidate-prototypes/hardware_first_center_rail_relief.json`

## Profile and observed fit

Each bottom rail keeps its original sloped 38.1 mm panel-bearing solid and
x = ±120 mm inner cut. A single 4×6 blank, nominally 90.4748 mm thick, is
profiled from its rear: only *added* material within 1 mm expanded bounding
boxes of the raised center header, same-side upper HL33 seat, and same-side
upper header bore is removed. This is one machined timber per rail, not a
lamination, lap joint, or custom steel part. The 1 mm envelope is a CAD
screening clearance, not a machining tolerance or installation allowance.

The resulting relief removes 211,236.362 mm³ on each side relative to the
unrelieved 4×6 model. Both
rails remain single CAD solids. Their sampled blank envelopes remain
90.4748 × 139.7 mm in section and 1010.3 mm (left) or 1007.125 mm (right)
in length, within the listed 90.4748 × 142.875 × 3048 mm dimensional
comparator. Delivered stock, grade, defects, and machining remain unverified.

The left x = −294.5 to −231 mm and right x = 231 to 294.5 mm rail-side
contact bands each retain the full 90.4748 mm one-piece section, above the
88.9 mm minimum stipulated for any proposed factory HL bracket seat. Their
63.5 mm lengths use the listed HL33 `L` as a *candidate contact-band
comparator*. No installed HL33 pose or mating header seat is selected by
this band check.

Measured positive-volume collisions with other wood, fixed panels, the
modeled upper seats and header bores, and the twelve existing frame-axis
envelopes are zero. The original bottom-rail wood is fully contained, all
66 panel/kicker screw axes retain their original receiver volume, and no
added rail wood intersects those screw envelopes. Fixed panel solids and
the source frame axes are unchanged. These are nominal occupancy results,
not fastener or bearing checks.

## Connection gate

The fit does not establish a factory bracket spanning rail and header, a
supported mating seat, correct catalog hole locations, bolt heads/nuts and
washers, tool and assembly access, edge/end distances, wood net section,
or connection load path and capacity. In particular, the remaining inner
end-face shape and the 63.5 mm full-section band are not an approved HL33
installation. No bracket loads or capacities are inferred. No drilling or
cutting is approved by this trial.
