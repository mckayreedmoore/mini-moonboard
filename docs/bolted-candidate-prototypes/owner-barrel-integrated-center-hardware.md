The replan places the nominal tips **25.349 mm** (5 in) and **12.649 mm**
(4½ in) beyond its *assumed* barrel thread axis, with a 1.651 mm trial washer.
At the modeled 10.0076 mm barrel OD, these tips are about **20.345 mm** and
**7.645 mm beyond the barrel's far wall**, respectively. The wood bore extends
another nominal 4 mm beyond each tip, but this geometry only works if the
delivered barrel's radial thread is open/through at the far side and its
thread/runout permits passage. Neither property is verified by the listing.
That is shaft reach, not engaged thread; even “fully threaded” is not a
controlled tip/runout or barrel-thread tolerance. The separate
[retail thread note](owner-barrel-retail-thread-evidence.md) does not qualify
these newly angled center paths. Neither A307 nor “All-purpose” assigns the
barrel a grade or supplies complete-joint capacity.

## Dry 4×6 and fallback

The modeled integrated posts are **88.9 × 139.7 mm**. The ordinary
[Lowe's #2 & Better Douglas-fir 4×6×8, item 92730](https://www.lowes.com/pd/4-in-x-6-in-x-8-ft-Douglas-Fir-Lumber-Common-3-562-in-x-5-625-in-x-8-ft-Actual/1000028917)
is **green** and listed about 90.5 × 142.9 mm; price and local stock require
location. A seemingly dry alternative,
[Lowe's #2 Southern-yellow-pine treated 4×6×8, item 314385](https://www.lowes.com/pd/Severe-Weather-Common-4-in-x-6-in-x-8-ft-Actual-3-5-in-x-5-5-in-x-8-ft-2-Treated-Lumber/4222527),
lists 3.5 × 5.5 in and “kiln-dried,” but its [product Q&A](https://www.lowes.com/questions/severe-weather-y240608-gc-pressure-treated-lumber/4222527/725a548b-c131-53e9-9e44-a3e6f64f8a50)
says **dried before treatment, not KDAT**. Delivered dryness is therefore not
established; species and treatment also change the material basis. Its price
and local stock are not public without location. **No exact dry, grade-stamped
structural 4×6 retail substitute was verified** in this bounded search.

If one bolt is absent, the other store's same nominal length is an identity
lead only; check its delivered threads, head, washer and grade in the actual
joint. Do not replace 4½ in with 5 in by convenience. If 880543 is absent at
Lowe's, Home Depot lists that *same model*; a different barrel requires a new
bore, axis, tolerance and resistance study. If neither washer fits, revise and
recheck the seat/bearing geometry. If dry 4×6 is unavailable, leave the wood
qualification open rather than treating green or pre-treatment-dried stock as
dry. No purchase, drilling or structural release follows from these leads.
