The six parts entail six crosscuts in this example: three on 4×4, one
on 2×4, two on 4×6. The upright-side cleat entails one lengthwise rip;
the principal cleat entails two sizing rips along its 145-mm grain-Y length.
From an exactly 88.9 × 139.7-mm blank and 3.2-mm kerf per rip, the
principal cleat's nominal offcut widths are 14.75 mm in X and 33.5 mm in Z.
With an illustrative 3.2-mm kerf **per separated piece**, no end trim,
defect allowance, planing, or squaring, and 8 ft = 2438.4 mm:

| First-checkout stick | Part lengths + crosscut kerfs, mm | Consumed / uncut remainder, mm |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| One 4×4×8 ft | 238.9 + 183 + 128.9 + 3×3.2 | 560.4 / 1878.0 |
| One 2×4×8 ft | 460 + 3.2 | 463.2 / 1975.2 |
| One 4×6×8 ft | 238.9 + 145 + 2×3.2 | 390.3 / 2048.1 |

One 8-ft stick of each nominal section has enough **length** for these
cuts. At exactly 88.9-mm blank depth, the 183-mm rip to 56.8 mm leaves
28.9 mm nominal offcut width after a 3.2-mm blade kerf. Crosscut remnants
and rip offcut are not credited to another part or priced as salvage. A
listed dimension is not a delivered minimum or saw setup. If suitable
4×6 stock is already owned, first checkout changes; the example assumes
one newly purchased 4×6 stick supplies both the backer and principal cleat.
