The backer values follow its 3-in shaft, 2.2 mm tip-past-axis and the same
5.0038 mm modeled barrel radius; they are outside the 48-row audit. A positive
shaft/OD intersection is neither a count of engaged turns nor proof that the
tip is in the dowel's *threaded* cross-hole. Tip bottoming, minimum engagement,
bolt length and runout tolerances, axis placement, and actual bore clearance
remain unknown for **all 52** pairs. In particular neither the 6-in rail nor
3-in backer family can be called engaged from the present public evidence.
