# HL formed-steel resistance: public-source limit

Status: **no complete no-contact HL33/HL53 formed-angle resistance input has
been established.** This is a source audit, not a claim that the product is
weak or that all arrangements fail.

The [2026 Simpson wood-connector catalog][catalog], HL table on p. 315,
identifies 7-gauge HL33/HL53 geometry, the specified through-bolt counts,
and DF/SP uplift/F1 allowable loads labeled (160). It does not state an
HL33/HL53-specific ASTM steel specification, guaranteed yield/tensile
strength, minimum delivered base-metal thickness, bend strength, governing
test mode, ordinary-duration (100) load, transverse F2 value, or moment
value. Its [general notes][general] say steel is individually selected for
each product and direct readers to Simpson for product-specific steel
information. The owner has ruled out manufacturer inquiries; none has been
made.

An ASTM A307 Grade A bolt value and a 2024 NDS Douglas-fir-larch or
southern-pine wood connection calculation can bound **those components**
once the joint geometry, species, moisture, load duration, bearing and
yield modes are known. Neither establishes the formed HL angle's net-section,
hole bearing/tearout, bending, or deflection limit. Likewise, the grade of
*another* Simpson connector or a generic 7-gauge steel chart cannot be
assigned to this exact HL production part. The reported (160) HL table value
cannot simply be divided by 1.6 to obtain a verified ordinary-duration
assembly value; the [duration decision](hl-ordinary-duration-decision.md)
explains the unreported governing mode.

**No-contact route forward:** search for an exact-model public evaluation
report, engineering letter, or catalog supplement that supplies the missing
material/mode and ordinary-duration information for the installed direction;
or use a different factory bracket with publicly documented applicability to
the actual joint. If one is found, combine its limit with wood, bolt, contact,
access, and simultaneous-action checks for the *same* built geometry. Until
then, HL geometry prototypes remain development evidence only. The absence
of a found public sheet is not a complete joint verdict, a fabrication
blocker for unrelated work, or a reason to invent an angle strength.
