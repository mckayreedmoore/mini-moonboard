# Thick six-rail screen for the center-tongue concept

**Status: rejected installed geometry trial.** This is a bounded nominal CAD
screen of the six cut rails in `hardware_first_center_tongue`, with its center
post, tongue, header, principals, plates, and bores retained. It adds no rail
bracket. The paired JSON is generated by:

`uv run python -m scripts.hardware_first_center_thick_rails --output docs/bolted-candidate-prototypes/hardware_first_center_thick_rails.json`

## Orientation and dimensional comparator

Each rail remains a single solid with its inner cut at x = ±120 mm. Its
original sloped front panel-bearing face stays fixed. The 38.1 mm section is
extended only toward the rear, in the negative Y and negative Z direction
normal to that face, to 90.4748 mm. This is 1.5748 mm above the stated
88.9 mm HL-family one-piece receiver minimum. The six sampled blank
envelopes are 90.4748 × 139.7 × 1010.3 mm (left) or 1007.125 mm (right).
They fit a *dimensional comparator* of 90.4748 × 142.875 × 3048 mm, the
listed actual 4×6 section with a 10-ft length allowance. This does not
verify delivered stock, machining allowance, availability, or member grade.
Larger sections are not needed to meet the nominal dimensional comparator.
With this fixed front face and rearward orientation, any thicker section
contains the rejected 4×6 envelope, so it cannot clear these clashes.

## Rejection

Both thickened bottom rails positively overlap the center-tongue header
(174,653.977 mm³ each), upper seat plate (22,089.850 mm³ each), and upper
header bore envelope (3,974.370 mm³ each). A 1 mm inward prism at each
bottom rail's x = ±120 mm end face is also blocked by the header and upper
seat. Therefore thickness alone does **not** retain accessible candidate end
faces for all six rails. The screen rejects this orientation promptly; no
alternative bracket pose is inferred.

All six original rail solids remain inside their thickened solids, so their
front panel-bearing surfaces are geometrically retained. The six fixed panel
solids have no positive-volume overlap with the changed rails; no original
panel-screw receiver volume is lost. All 66 panel/kicker screw axes and 12
existing frame axes remain at their source coordinates. Existing intended
screw penetration into a rail is excluded from the *new interference* check;
the added rail wood creates no additional screw-axis collision. The changed
rails have no frame-axis collision. These observations do not establish
bearing adequacy, screw performance, connector access, or a rated joint.

The modeled tongue-concept plates and bores are ideal occupancy envelopes.
No rail bracket, bolt stack, head/nut/washer space, tool path, end/edge
distance, capacity, or drilling coordinates are selected or qualified here.
