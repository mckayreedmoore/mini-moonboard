# MiTek B66 / retail UB66: one rejected center geometry trial

This is one nominal installed fit screen in the separate bolted lane. It is
not a connector selection, structural rating, purchase instruction, or drill
plan. The companion JSON is reproduced with the script's `--output` option,
targeting `docs/bolted-candidate-prototypes/hardware_first_b66_center.json`.

The model uses the official MiTek DXF's four circle centers documented in
`mitek-b66-drawing-followup.md`: two nominal free-end offsets of about 25.4
and 126.744 mm on each leg. The approximate 10.3-mm DXF circle diameter
screens ideal full bores; it is not a wood bit or delivered-hole tolerance.
An illustrative 2.66-mm sheet represents the plates, without a verified bend
or coating envelope. Four factory B66 braces use removable through-bolts;
the members meet by butt contact. There are no lap joints or custom steel.

One solid post has its front flush with both fixed kicker backs. Two lower
braces attach to its rear Y face, with seats under a deeper, rear-extended
header. Two upper braces attach to the center principals' outer X faces,
with seats atop the header. The selected climbing face, kerf-right panel
outlines, and all 66 protected panel/kicker screw axes remain fixed. Both
kicker inner edges have nominal post backing, and all 66 historical occupied
screw cylinders intersect their assigned receiving timber. This is CAD
occupancy, not screw embedment or backing strength qualification.

The pose is rejected. The lower bolt rows at X = ±70 mm leave only
**22.075 mm** from each center to the post's outer X edge, below the
conservative **4D = 38.1 mm** reversible loaded-edge search filter for a
3/8-in bolt. This is a screen, not a formal NDS edge-distance verdict.
The full nominal upper vertical bore at each first principal lacks about
7,407 mm³ of wood at the oblique toe. The enlarged header intersects both
side timbers, each widened principal intersects three neighboring rails,
and each upper vertical plate intersects a bottom rail. Distinct brackets
do not collide; each angle's own joined vertical and seat plates are excluded
from that check. No plate or bore intersects a protected panel or occupied
panel/kicker screw cylinder in this nominal pose.

One-piece stock is a separate feasibility blocker. The ideal post blank
bounding envelope is **184.15 × 139.7 × 238.9 mm**, implying a nominal 6×8
class cross section. A public Home Depot listing was found for a **6×8×8
Douglas-fir pressure-treated** product, a different wood/service basis; no ordinary
untreated DF-L No. 2 big-box lead is established. The full-span header needs
an ideal **2435.23 × 292.1 × 76.2 mm** bounding blank, with ordinary
untreated DF-L No. 2 availability also unverified. Each widened principal
retains a full-member bounding envelope of **88.9 × 1693.79 × 1940.1 mm**;
the YZ bounding diagonal is 2575.45 mm, not a cut length. These dimensions
are ideal envelope checks, not stock specifications or fabrication data.

Two smaller posts and separate seam backing would be another trial, with at
least one additional backing-to-post connection to count and qualify. It
would have to keep the fixed X = ±70 kicker screw axes and both seam edges
supported. This trial does not establish that alternative. Existing frame
bolt axes after hidden-member changes, washer/head/nut/grip stacks, tool
access and removal, delivered bracket variation, and local retail stock
have not been checked. No capacity is inferred from ESR-3455's `C_D = 1.6`
table. No cutting or drilling follows from this rejected pose.
