# 3-D contact output coupons

These tiny Code_Aster 17.4 fixtures isolate contact result fields. They are method checks, not joint response, member resistance, or candidate acceptance.

Both variants use two matching PENTA15 blocks per side and two matching TRIA6 surface elements over a 100 mm square, split along diagonal A–C. The master occupies `z=-20..0 mm`; the slave starts at a 0.02 mm gap and extends 20 mm above its contact face. All nodes have `DX=DY=0`; the master cut is fixed at `DZ=0`; the slave cut is displaced to `DZ=-0.03 mm`. Units are mm, N, and MPa, with `E=1000 MPa` and `nu=0.25`.

The flat variant keeps both slave triangles in `z=0.02 mm`. It is a uniform fully active contact patch. The imposed closure first removes the 0.02 mm initial gap, then compresses the two 20 mm solids by 0.01 mm in total. The exact plane-strain axial modulus is 1200 MPa, so the reference pressure magnitude is 0.3 MPa and the 10,000 mm² patch resultant is 3,000 N. It extracts `DEPL/LAGS_C`, `CONT_NOEU/RN`, `SIEF_NOEU/SIZZ`, and both cut `FORC_NODA` and `REAC_NODA` vectors. `FORC_NODA` is assembled from element stresses; `REAC_NODA` is the independent constrained-cut reaction field. This is a 3-D check of continuous-contact output and equilibrium for a flat, fully active, conforming interface.

The mixed variant raises slave face B (`A–C–D`) by 5 mm from its shared edge with face A (`A–B–C`). In the first mixed run, the active face is ordered before the open face. The six `AUTO` quadrature samples on face B start at least 0.4779 mm from the flat master; even a hypothetical uniform 0.03 mm approach leaves more than 0.4479 mm clearance. This is a targeted diagnostic for order-sensitive writes to shared-node `CONT_NOEU` values when one face is active and its neighbor is open. A matched control reverses only the two entries in the `SLAVE` group, placing the open face first and the active face last. There is no analytic force magnitude for this mixed geometry; compare RN against the equilibrated cut reactions and use the control to test output-order dependence, not capacity or acceptance.

The PENTA15 ordering is the v17 Code_Aster order: first cap nodes 1–3 and midsides 7–9; second cap 4–6 and midsides 13–15; longitudinal midsides 10–12. Contact skins are explicit TRIA6s with the master face oriented toward +Z and the slave faces outward toward −Z. The offline preflight checks connectivity, line widths, group order, positive prism orientation, shared-node count, surface orientation, and the mixed-face quadrature gap margin.

The v17 contact guide defines `LAGS_C` as a contact-force density on the reference configuration and says continuous contact exposes it in `DEPL`. The guide documents `CONT_NOEU` with AUTO integration and its global `RNX/RNY/RNZ` normal-force components. `CALC_CHAMP` documents `FORC_NODA` as stress-derived nodal force and `REAC_NODA` as reaction output. Sources: [continuous formulation and LAGS_C](https://code-aster.org/doc/v17/manuals/man_u/u2/u2.04.04/R_solution.html), [RN and contact postprocessing](https://code-aster.org/doc/v17/manuals/man_u/u2/u2.04.04/M_thodologies.html), [CALC_CHAMP](https://code-aster.org/doc/v17/manuals/man_u/u4/u4.81.04/index.html), [Code_Aster ASTER mesh format](https://demo-docaster.simvia-app.fr/versions/v17/manuals/man_u/u3/u3.01.00/index.html), and upstream v17.4 [quadratic 3-D continuous-contact test SSNP121R](https://gitlab.com/codeaster/src/-/raw/17.4.0/astest/ssnp121r.comm).

Run `generate_mesh.py` with `--rise-mm 0` or `--rise-mm 5`, then run `preflight.py` before freezing inputs. The frozen attempt checker is offline; it does not invoke Code_Aster. Parent owns readiness review, serialized native execution, and interpretation.
